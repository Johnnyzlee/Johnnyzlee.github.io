Research Watchlist

Projects

  • orbital-compute [Live Demo] — An open-source simulator for scheduling GPU compute jobs across satellite constellations.

  • awesome-satellite-imagery-datasets — A curated list of aerial and satellite imagery datasets for computer vision and deep learning (archived).

  • MobiCom24-SatelliteCOTS [Paper] — Datasets and analysis tools from real-satellite measurements of COTS computing devices, covering thermal and energy behavior.

  • LeoCC [Paper] — A congestion-control algorithm designed to remain robust under LEO satellite network dynamics.

  • LEOCraft [Paper] — A modular flow-level simulator for evaluating LEO constellation throughput, latency/stretch, traffic flow, and coverage.
