Space Computing Power Networks

Research Watchlist

Projects

  • orbital-compute (2026) — An open-source simulator for evaluating orbital GPU computing across mechanics, power, thermal, networking, scheduling, and economics. [Repository] [Live Demo]

  • LeoCC (2025) — A reconfiguration-aware congestion-control system that remains robust to fast-changing LEO paths. [Repository] [Paper]

  • LEOCraft (2025) — A modular flow-level simulator for rapid evaluation of LEO constellation throughput, latency, routing, and coverage. [Repository] [Documentation] [Paper]

  • Xingkan Satellite Digital Twin (2026) — An interactive digital-twin system for visualizing global satellite constellations using real-time and historical TLE data. [Live Demo]

  • PASEOS (2024) — A decentralized Python environment simulator for spacecraft operations under power, thermal, radiation, orbit, and communication constraints. [Repository] [Documentation] [Paper]

  • LeoEM (2023) — A real-time emulator that turns time-varying LEO topology and routes into native Linux network paths for end-to-end experiments. [Repository] [Paper]

Datasets

  • satellite-image-deep-learning/datasets (2022) — A living catalog of satellite and aerial imagery datasets for deep learning, organized by sensor, platform, and task. [Repository]

  • TorchGeo Datasets (2021) — A maintained collection of geospatial datasets with PyTorch-ready loaders, metadata-aware composition, and benchmark support. [Repository] [Documentation] [Paper]

  • MobiCom24-SatelliteCOTS (2024) — Real in-orbit telemetry and experiment code for studying the thermal and energy behavior of COTS computing payloads on BUPT-1. [Repository] [Paper]

  • LENS (2024) — A multi-location, longitudinal Starlink measurement dataset with raw and processed network-performance snapshots. [Repository] [Dataset Portal] [Paper]

  • WetLinks (2024) — A longitudinal Starlink dataset that aligns throughput, latency, routing, terminal telemetry, and contiguous weather observations. [Repository] [Paper]

  • ESA Anomalies Dataset & ESA-ADB (2024) — A three-mission satellite-telemetry dataset with curated anomaly annotations and a reproducible evaluation benchmark. [Official Page] [Repository] [Data] [Benchmark] [Paper] [Preprint]

  • OPSSAT-AD (2024) — An AI-ready anomaly-detection benchmark built from annotated telemetry segments collected aboard ESA's OPS-SAT CubeSat. [Repository] [Data] [Paper] [Preprint]
