Random

From time to time I add things to this page that I came across in my daily browsing and found worth keeping. My criteria have never been fully consistent, but one thing has always been true: every link here is something that, at the moment I read it, stirred something real in me — agreement, admiration, awe, or something harder to name. Not content that an AI judged to be good writing or good thinking, but content that my own living self actually responded to.

I am aware that, in an age where AI can produce and evaluate “good” content effortlessly, much of what I keep here may turn out to be wrong, short-sighted, or of no great aesthetic merit. That does not bother me. This page was never meant to be a curated collection of quality. It is a record of moments — traces of the person I was as I walked through the world, one small resonance at a time. I may never open most of these links again, but I still want them here, because seeing this trail gives me a quiet sense of satisfaction. It is a positive feeling.

One day I will leave this academic world behind — all the knowledge I once studied, all the developments I felt I had to keep up with, all the traces of technology. When that day comes, I believe the page I will treasure most is this one. It tracks the most honest and free version of myself. I want to protect that self.
