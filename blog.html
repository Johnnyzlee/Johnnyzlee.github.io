Li Zeng's Blogs

The blog section organizes structured knowledge at the frontier of what I understand, in two kinds. First, knowledge I have already mastered, written down to organize and deepen it — writing sharpens my own grasp, and the finished record shows exactly how far I have gotten, so when I bring a question to an AI it can see the edge of my understanding and answer precisely: never re-explaining what I already know, and never leaping to what stands so far beyond my reach that it would slip into hollow talk; I find this a genuinely solid way to improve oneself in the age of AI. Second, knowledge I have not yet fully absorbed: focused, self-contained tutorials that let me learn efficiently, one step at a time — where the Study page builds the complete towers of a discipline, these are the smaller scaffolds I raise near where I actually stand.
