A. Brain Teasers

Z001 · Screwy pirates / 古怪海盗分金 （Page 19）

中文: 五名海盗分 100 枚金币。最年长者先提出方案，所有人（包括提案者）投票；赞成票达到至少一半时方案通过，否则提案者被处死，再由下一位提出方案。海盗依次追求：先保命、再多拿金币；若两种结果在这两点上相同，则更愿意看到其他海盗被处死。最终金币会怎样分配？

English: Five pirates must divide 100 gold coins. The most senior proposes a division and everyone votes. A proposal passes with at least 50% of the votes; otherwise its proposer is killed and the process repeats. Each pirate ranks survival first, then coins, then—if still indifferent—prefers another pirate to die. What division results?

Solution

Work backward. With two pirates, the senior pirate's own vote is enough, so he keeps all 100. With three, the senior pirate buys the vote of the pirate who would otherwise receive zero for one coin. Repeating this argument, with five pirates the most senior buys the votes of pirates 1 and 3 for one coin each.

The final allocation from most senior to least senior is $(98,0,1,0,1)$. It passes $3$–$2$.

Z002 · Tiger and sheep / 老虎与羊 （Page 20）

中文: 魔法岛上有 100 只老虎和 1 只羊，岛上只有草。老虎能吃草，但更想吃羊；每次只能有一只老虎吃羊，而且吃掉羊的老虎随后会变成羊。所有老虎都足够聪明，并把生存放在首位。这只羊会被吃掉吗？

English: One hundred perfectly rational tigers and one sheep live on an island. A tiger prefers sheep to grass, but a tiger that eats the sheep becomes a sheep and values survival above eating. Only one tiger can eat the sheep at a time. Will the sheep be eaten?

Solution

Backward induction on the number of tigers gives an alternating result. With one tiger, it eats; with two, either eater would become a sheep and be eaten, so neither eats. Thus an odd number eats and an even number does not: after eating, an odd population leaves an even tiger population, in which the new sheep survives.

Because 100 is even, the original sheep is not eaten.

Z003 · River crossing / 过桥问题 （Page 21）

中文: A、B、C、D 四人夜间过桥，过桥必须带唯一的一支手电，桥上每次最多两人，同行速度取决于较慢者。四人单独过桥分别需要 10、5、2、1 分钟。让所有人到达对岸至少需要多少时间？

English: Four people take 10, 5, 2, and 1 minutes to cross a bridge. At most two may cross together, they must carry the only torch, and a pair moves at the slower person's speed. What is the minimum time for all four to cross?

Solution

The optimal sequence is: the 1- and 2-minute people cross (2); the 1-minute person returns (1); the 5- and 10-minute people cross (10); the 2-minute person returns (2); then the 1- and 2-minute people cross (2). Total: 17 minutes.

The two slowest must cross together; otherwise the required torch returns make their combined cost no smaller. This gives the matching lower bound.

Z004 · Birthday problem / 生日推理 （Page 21）

中文: 老板的生日只可能是以下十个日期之一：3 月 4、5、8 日；6 月 4、7 日；9 月 1、5 日；12 月 1、2、8 日。你只知道月份，同事 C 只知道日期。你先说：“我不知道，C 也不知道。”C 随后说：“原来不知道，现在知道了。”你再说：“现在我也知道了。”秘书听完便写下答案。老板的生日是哪一天？

English: A birthday is one of: Mar 4, 5, 8; Jun 4, 7; Sep 1, 5; Dec 1, 2, 8. You know only the month and C knows only the day. You say, ‘I do not know, and C does not know either.’ C then says, ‘Now I know.’ You reply, ‘Now I know too.’ What is the birthday?

Solution

Your first statement rules out June and December: on day 7 or day 2, respectively, C could have known immediately. The remaining dates are Mar 4, Mar 5, Mar 8, Sep 1, Sep 5. C's new certainty rules out day 5, leaving Mar 4, Mar 8, Sep 1. If your month were March you would still be uncertain, so it must be September.

The birthday is September 1.

Z005 · Card game / 红黑牌游戏 （Page 22）

中文: 一副 52 张牌每次翻两张：两张黑牌归庄家，两张红牌归你，一红一黑则丢弃，直到牌翻完。你的牌数多于庄家便赢 100 美元，否则（含平局）收益为零。你最多愿意付多少钱参加这场游戏？

English: A shuffled 52-card deck is exposed two cards at a time. A black-black pair goes to the dealer, a red-red pair goes to you, and a mixed pair is discarded. You receive USD 100 only if your pile ends larger than the dealer's. What is a fair entry price?

Solution

Let $RR$, $BB$, and $RB$ be the numbers of red-red, black-black, and mixed pairs. Counting red and black cards gives $2RR+RB=26=2BB+RB$, hence $RR=BB$. Your pile and the dealer's pile always have equal size.

You can never win under the stated payoff, so the fair entry price is zero.

Z006 · Burning ropes / 燃烧的绳子 （Page 23）

中文: 有两根材质不均匀的绳子，每根从一端烧完都恰好需要 1 小时，但各段燃烧速度不恒定。怎样用它们准确计时 45 分钟？

English: Two nonuniform ropes each take exactly one hour to burn from one end, but their local burn rates vary. How can they measure exactly 45 minutes?

Solution

At time zero, light both ends of rope A and one end of rope B. Rope A finishes after 30 minutes. At that instant light the other end of rope B. Its remaining burn time is then 15 minutes, so rope B finishes exactly 45 minutes after the start.

Z007 · Defective ball / 瑕疵球称重 （Page 23）

中文: 12 个外观相同的球中有 1 个重量异常，但不知道它更重还是更轻。只用一架能比较两边轻重的天平，怎样在 3 次称量内找出异常球并判断它是重是轻？

English: Among 12 visually identical balls, one has a different weight and may be either heavier or lighter. Using a balance scale, identify the ball and whether it is heavy or light in at most three weighings.

Solution

Label the balls 1–12. First weigh 1,2,3,4 against 5,6,7,8. If they balance, weigh 9,10,11 against three known-normal balls. A balance makes 12 defective; otherwise the direction tells whether one of 9–11 is heavy or light, and weighing 9 against 10 resolves it.

If the first left pan is heavy, the candidates are 1–4 heavy or 5–8 light. Weigh 1,2,5 against 3,6,9, where 9 is normal. If the left pan is heavy, test 1 against 2: the heavier one is defective, or a balance means 6 is light. If the right pan is heavy, test 3 against 9: 3 heavy or, on balance, 5 light. If they balance, test 7 against 8: the lighter one is defective, or a balance means 4 is heavy. Mirror the labels when the first right pan is heavy.

Z008 · Trailing zeros / 阶乘末尾零 （Page 25）

中文: $100!$ 的末尾有多少个零？

English: How many trailing zeros does $100!$ have?

Solution

Each trailing zero requires a factor 5 paired with a factor 2, and factors 2 are abundant. The number of factors $5$ in $100!$ is $\lfloor100/5\rfloor+\lfloor100/25\rfloor=20+4=24$.

Z009 · Horse race / 赛马选优 （Page 25）

中文: 25 匹马速度各不相同且保持恒定；赛道一次最多容纳 5 匹马，也没有秒表。要找出最快的 3 匹马，最少需要赛几场？

English: Twenty-five horses have distinct constant speeds. A race has at most five horses and no stopwatch is available. What is the minimum number of races needed to identify the three fastest horses?

Solution

Race five groups of five, then race the five group winners: six races. Suppose the winners finish $A_1>B_1>C_1>D_1>E_1$, and each group was internally ordered. $A_1$ is fastest overall. Only $A_2,A_3,B_1,B_2,C_1$ can still be second or third. Race those five once.

Seven races suffice. Six cannot suffice because after the group-winner race the remaining candidates for second and third have not been mutually ordered.

Z010 · Infinite sequence / 无穷序列 （Page 26）

中文: 若无穷幂塔 $x^{x^{x^{\cdot^{\cdot^\cdot}}}}$ 的值为 $2$，求 $x$。

English: If the infinite power tower $x^{x^{x^{\cdot^{\cdot^\cdot}}}}$ converges to $2$, what is $x$?

Solution

Let the convergent tower equal $L$. Then $L=x^L$. With $L=2$, $x^2=2$, so $x=\sqrt2$ for the positive real tower. This value lies in the real convergence interval for infinite power towers, so the proposed limit is consistent.

Z011 · Box packing / 装箱问题 （Page 26）

中文: 能否把 53 块尺寸为 $1\times1\times4$ 的砖完整装进一个 $6\times6\times6$ 的立方体箱子？

English: Can 53 bricks of size $1\times1\times4$ be packed into a $6\times6\times6$ box without overlap?

Solution

Partition the box into $27$ blocks of size $2\times2\times2$ and checkerboard-color these blocks. There are 14 blocks of one color and 13 of the other. Every axis-aligned $1\times1\times4$ brick occupies two unit layers in each of two adjacent, oppositely colored blocks. A $2\times2\times2$ block can accommodate at most four such half-bricks.

The color represented by only 13 blocks therefore supports at most $13\times4=52$ bricks. Packing 53 is impossible.

Z012 · Calendar cubes / 日历方块 （Page 27）

中文: 定制两个六面方块，在每个面写一个数字。每天用两个方块的正面显示当月日期，1—9 日必须显示成 01—09，两个方块可以互换位置。两个方块的各个面应分别写哪些数字，才能显示 01—31？

English: Put one digit on each face of two cubes so that, using both cubes and allowing their order to swap, their front faces can display every calendar date 01 through 31. Which digits should be used?

Solution

One valid labeling is cube A: 0,1,2,3,4,5; cube B: 0,1,2,6,7,8. Turn the 6 upside down to represent 9. The duplicated 0, 1, and 2 allow 01–09, 11, 22, and all tens digits needed through 31.

Z013 · Door to offer / 门与录用通知 （Page 28）

中文: 面前有两扇门，一扇通向录用通知，另一扇通向出口；两名守卫一人永远说真话，一人永远说谎。只能向其中一人问一个是非问题。应问什么，才能确定通往录用通知的门？

English: Two doors lead respectively to a job offer and the exit. One guard always lies and the other always tells the truth. You may ask either guard one yes/no question. How do you find the offer?

Solution

Ask either guard: ‘Which door would the other guard say leads to the offer?’ Both guards indicate the wrong door—one by reporting the liar's answer truthfully and the other by lying about the truthful answer. Choose the opposite door.

Z014 · Message delivery / 消息传递 （Page 29）

中文: 你要通过不可信的信使把文件寄给同事。文件可以放进带挂锁的箱子；你和同事各自的锁都只有上锁者持有唯一钥匙，而未上锁的箱子内物品在途中会丢失。怎样在不交换钥匙的情况下安全送达文件？

English: You and a colleague each own a padlock with a unique private key. An insecure courier will steal anything sent in an unlocked box. How can you send a document securely without exchanging keys?

Solution

Send the locked box using your lock. Your colleague adds their own lock and sends it back. Remove your lock and return the box, still secured by the colleague's lock. The colleague removes that lock with their key. The box is locked during every trip and no key is transmitted.

Z015 · Last ball / 最后一球 （Page 29）

中文: 袋中起初有 20 个蓝球和 14 个红球。每次随机取出两个球且不放回：同色时放入一个蓝球，异色时放入一个红球；不断重复直到只剩一个球。最后一个球是什么颜色？如果初始改为 20 个蓝球和 13 个红球，答案又是什么？

English: A bag starts with 20 blue and 14 red balls. Repeatedly remove two balls: replace a same-color pair with one blue ball and a mixed pair with one red ball. What is the final ball's color? What changes if the bag starts with 20 blue and 13 red balls?

Solution

Track the parity of the number of red balls. Removing two same-color balls changes the red count by 0 or −2; removing a mixed pair and adding red also leaves the red count unchanged. Thus red parity is invariant. With one ball left, an even red count means the last ball is blue, while an odd red count means it is red.

Starting with 14 red gives a blue final ball; starting with 13 red gives a red final ball.

Z016 · Light switches / 灯与开关 （Page 30）

中文: 房间内有一盏灯，房间外有四个开关，只有一个开关控制这盏灯，起初全部关闭。你可以任意操作开关。最少进入房间几次，才能确定正确的开关？

English: One of four outside switches controls a bulb inside a closed room; all switches start off. You may operate the switches freely. What is the fewest number of visits to the room needed to identify the controlling switch?

Solution

One visit suffices if both light and heat can be observed. Turn switches 1 and 2 on long enough to heat the bulb. Then turn switch 2 off, turn switch 3 on, and immediately enter the room. A lit and hot bulb identifies switch 1; off and hot identifies switch 2; lit and cold identifies switch 3; off and cold identifies switch 4.

Z017 · Quant salary / 量化薪资 （Page 31）

中文: 来自不同银行的 8 名量化从业者想算出大家的平均薪资，但每个人都不愿向其他人透露自己的工资。设计一种能得到平均值、又不会让任何人知道他人工资的办法。

English: Eight quants want to compute their average salary while no participant reveals an individual salary to anyone else. Give a protocol that computes the average.

Solution

Quant 1 privately chooses a random offset $R$, adds it to their salary, and sends the result to quant 2. Each subsequent quant adds their own salary and passes the running total; quant 8 returns it to quant 1. Quant 1 subtracts $R$ and announces the total, which everyone divides by eight.

No participant sees another salary in isolation. This protects against individual observers, though not against arbitrary collusion between neighbors.

Z018 · Coin piles / 硬币堆对称性 （Page 31）

中文: 你蒙着眼面对 1000 枚硬币，其中 980 枚反面朝上、20 枚正面朝上。你无法靠触摸辨认正反面，但可以翻转任意硬币。怎样把硬币分成两堆，并保证两堆中正面朝上的硬币数相同？

English: While blindfolded, you have 1,000 coins: 980 tails-up and 20 heads-up. You cannot feel orientation but may flip coins. Divide them into two piles guaranteed to contain the same number of heads.

Solution

Take any 20 coins as pile A and put the other 980 in pile B. If A initially contains $h$ heads, then B contains $20-h$ heads. Flip every coin in A; its $h$ heads become tails and its $20-h$ tails become heads. Both piles now contain $20-h$ heads.

Z019 · Mislabeled bags / 标签全错的袋子 （Page 32）

中文: 三个袋子分别装苹果、橙子以及苹果橙子混合物，袋上的“苹果”“橙子”“混合”三个标签全部贴错。最少取出几个水果、应从哪个袋子取，才能确定每袋的内容？

English: Three bags contain only apples, only oranges, and a mixture. The labels ‘apples,’ ‘oranges,’ and ‘mix’ are all wrong. What is the minimum number of fruit draws needed to identify every bag?

Solution

Draw one fruit from the bag labeled ‘mix.’ Because every label is wrong, this bag is pure. If the draw is an apple, relabel it apples; the bag labeled oranges cannot be oranges and cannot now be apples, so it is mix, and the last is oranges. The orange case is symmetric.

Exactly one fruit draw is necessary and sufficient.

Z020 · Wise men / 智者与玻璃杯 （Page 33）

中文: 苏丹囚禁了 50 名智者，并放置一个初始正放、可以翻转的玻璃杯。每分钟随机叫出一人，此人可以翻转杯子或不操作；同一人可能被叫出无限多次。任何人一旦正确宣布“所有人都至少被叫过一次”，众人获释；若判断错误则全部处死。智者只能在被隔离前商议一次。请设计保证最终获释的策略。

English: Fifty prisoners are visited one at a time in random order, with repetitions, and see a glass initially upright. A visitor may flip it or do nothing. Before separation they may agree on a strategy; afterward, a correct declaration that everyone has visited frees all, while a false declaration kills all. Give a strategy that eventually succeeds with certainty.

Solution

Designate one prisoner as counter. Every other prisoner flips the glass from upright to upside down exactly once—the first time they see it upright—and otherwise does nothing. The counter flips it back upright whenever it is upside down and increments a private count. When the count reaches 49, the counter declares that everyone has visited.

Every counted flip came from a distinct non-counter. Random repeated visits ensure all 49 flips and counter resets eventually occur with probability one.

Z021 · Clock pieces / 时钟碎片求和 （Page 34）

中文: 一个按顺时针标有 1—12 的钟面摔成三个相连的碎片，每块碎片上的数字之和相同，且不允许形状离奇地跨越钟面。三块各包含哪些数字？

English: A clock face numbered 1 through 12 breaks into three ordinary connected pieces whose number sums are equal. Which numbers lie on each piece?

Solution

The total is $1+\cdots+12=78$, so each piece must sum to $26$. One geometrically feasible division is $\{11,12,1,2\}$, $\{3,4,9,10\}$, and $\{5,6,7,8\}$; every set sums to $26$.

Z022 · Missing integers / 缺失整数 （Page 34）

中文: 从 1 到 100 中取出 98 个互不相同的整数。怎样高效找出缺少的两个整数？

English: Given 98 distinct integers from 1 through 100, find the two missing integers efficiently.

Solution

Let the missing numbers be $a$ and $b$. From the sum, $s=a+b=5050-\sum x_i$. From the sum of squares, $q=a^2+b^2=338350-\sum x_i^2$. Then $ab=(s^2-q)/2$. The missing numbers are the two roots of $t^2-st+ab=0$.

This uses constant auxiliary storage and one pass through the 98 numbers.

Z023 · Counterfeit coins I / 假币问题一 （Page 35）

中文: A：有 10 袋硬币，每袋 100 枚。正常硬币每枚重 10 克，只有一袋是假币，且该袋每枚都重 9 克或都重 11 克，但不知道是哪一种。只用一次能显示精确重量的电子秤，怎样找出假币袋？

B：一栋楼有 100 层，两只完全相同的玻璃球从低于临界楼层 $X$ 的位置扔下不会破，从第 $X$ 层或更高处扔下必破。怎样在最坏情况下用最少的投掷次数确定 $X$？

English: A. Ten bags contain 100 coins each. Nine bags have 10-gram coins; one bag has coins all weighing either 9 or 11 grams. Find the counterfeit bag and its coin weight with one exact digital-scale weighing.

B. Two identical glass balls are tested in a 100-story building. A ball survives every drop below an unknown threshold floor $X$ and breaks from floor $X$ or above. Determine $X$ while minimizing the worst-case number of drops.

Solution

A. Take 1 coin from bag 1, 2 from bag 2, …, and 10 from bag 10. If all were genuine the weight would be $10(1+\cdots+10)=550$ grams. An observed deviation of $+k$ grams identifies bag $k$ as containing 11-gram coins; a deviation of $-k$ identifies bag $k$ as containing 9-gram coins.

B. If at most $n$ drops are allowed, choose the first-ball test floors with successively decreasing gaps $n,n-1,\ldots,1$. A break leaves at most the number of remaining drops minus one floors to test linearly with the second ball. Such a schedule covers at most

$$n+(n-1)+\cdots+1=\frac{n(n+1)}2$$

floors. Since $13\cdot14/2=91<100$ but $14\cdot15/2=105\ge100$, at least 14 drops are necessary. They are sufficient: test the first ball at floors $14,27,39,50,60,69,77,84,90,95,99$, then 100 if needed; after a break, test the unresolved floors one by one with the second ball. Every branch uses at most 14 drops.

Z024 · Matching socks / 配对袜子 （Page 37）

中文: 抽屉里有 2 只红袜、20 只黄袜和 31 只蓝袜。闭眼取袜子时，至少取多少只才能保证其中有一双同色袜子？

English: A drawer contains 2 red, 20 yellow, and 31 blue socks. How many socks must be drawn blindly to guarantee a same-color pair?

Solution

There are three colors. Three draws could contain one of each color, so they do not guarantee a pair. The fourth draw must repeat a color by the pigeonhole principle. The answer is 4.

Z025 · Handshakes / 握手次数 （Page 37）

中文: 一场聚会中除你以外还有 25 人，每个人都与你握手，其他人之间也可能彼此握手。即使不知道握手总次数，能否断定现场至少有两个人与完全相同数量的人握过手？请说明理由。

English: At a party with you and 25 teammates, everyone else shakes your hand and arbitrary additional handshakes occur. Prove that at least two people have shaken hands with the same number of people.

Solution

You have degree 25 in the handshake graph. Each of the other 25 people has degree between 1 and 25 because everyone shook your hand. If their degrees are not all distinct, the claim is proved. If they are all distinct, they must be exactly 1,…,25, so the person of degree 25 has the same degree as you. Either way a duplicate exists.

Z026 · Have we met before? / 是否曾经相遇 （Page 37）

中文: 证明：任意 6 人中，必定存在 3 人彼此认识，或者存在 3 人彼此都不认识。

English: Prove that among any six people there are either three mutual acquaintances or three mutual strangers.

Solution

Choose any person A. Among the other five, at least three are either acquaintances of A or strangers to A. Suppose B,C,D are acquaintances. If any pair among B,C,D know each other, that pair with A forms three mutual acquaintances; otherwise B,C,D are three mutual strangers. The other case is the same with ‘acquaintance’ and ‘stranger’ interchanged.

Z027 · Ants on a square / 正方形上的蚂蚁 （Page 38）

中文: 边长为 1 的正方形内有 51 只蚂蚁。用一个半径为 1/7 的圆形玻璃杯，能否总找到一种摆放位置，使杯口至少圈住 3 只蚂蚁？

English: Fifty-one ants lie in a unit square. Can a circular glass of radius 1/7 always be positioned to cover at least three ants?

Solution

Partition the square into a $5\times5$ grid. By the pigeonhole principle, one small square contains at least $\lceil51/25\rceil=3$ ants. A circle of radius 1/7 centered at that square's center covers it because the center-to-corner distance is $\sqrt2/10<1/7$. Therefore the glass can cover at least three ants.

Z028 · Counterfeit coins II / 假币问题二 （Page 38）

中文: 有 5 袋硬币，每袋 100 枚；一枚硬币可能重 9、10 或 11 克，同一袋内重量相同，但各袋属于哪一类未知。使用能显示精确重量的电子秤，最少称几次可以确定每袋硬币的重量？

English: Five bags contain 100 coins each. Every coin weighs 9, 10, or 11 grams, and each bag is internally uniform. Determine every bag's type using an exact digital scale as few times as possible.

Solution

One weighing is enough. Take $1,3,9,27,81$ coins from bags $1$–$5$. Relative to the $10$-gram baseline of $1{,}210$ grams, the deviation is $\sum_{i=1}^5 d_i3^{i-1}$, where $d_i\in\{-1,0,1\}$ according as bag $i$ contains $9$-, $10$-, or $11$-gram coins. Balanced-ternary representation is unique, so the single deviation identifies all five bags.

Z029 · Prisoner problem / 囚犯与彩帽 （Page 40）

中文: A：100 名囚犯各戴一顶随机分配的红帽或蓝帽；每人能看到别人的帽子，却看不到自己的。囚犯按随机顺序公开猜自己的帽色，猜对获释、猜错处死，开始后不能交流，但可事先商议。怎样制定策略，最多能保证救下多少人？

B：如果可能的帽色改为红、绿、蓝三种，最多又能保证救下多少人？

English: A. One hundred prisoners wear arbitrary red or blue hats. Each sees all other hats and, in random order, publicly guesses only their own color; a correct guess frees that prisoner and an incorrect guess kills them. They may agree on a strategy beforehand. How many can they guarantee to save?

B. What changes if the possible hat colors are red, green, and blue?

Solution

A. Whoever is called first acts as the parity announcer: that prisoner encodes the parity of the red hats among the other 99 in the color they announce. Every later prisoner combines that announced parity with the other 98 hats they can see to infer their own hat.

The first prisoner may be wrong, but every other prisoner is correct. Thus 99 can be guaranteed; 100 cannot be guaranteed because the first speaker receives no information distinguishing the two possible colors of their own hat.

B. Encode the three colors as $0,1,2$ modulo $3$. The first prisoner announces the color encoding the sum of the other 99 color values modulo $3$. Every later prisoner subtracts the visible sum of the other 98 from that announced residue, modulo $3$, thereby recovering their own color. Again 99 prisoners are guaranteed to survive; the first survives with probability $1/3$ under uniformly random hats.

It is $0$ when $y>z$ (and handle $z=0$ by the limiting convention). Also $E[Y]=1/3$, $E[Z]=2/3$, $E[YZ]=E[X_1X_2]=1/4$, and $\operatorname{Var}(Y)=\operatorname{Var}(Z)=1/18$. Hence $\operatorname{Corr}(Y,Z)=1/2$.

Z101 · Color balls / 彩球状态转移 （Page 129）

中文: 盒中有 $n$ 个球，初始颜色各不相同。每一步随机选两个球，把第一个球涂成第二个球的颜色，再把两球放回。直到所有球同色，所需步骤数的期望是多少？

English: A box initially contains $n$ balls of $n$ distinct colors. At each step an ordered pair is chosen uniformly, the first ball is repainted to the second ball's color, and both are returned. Find the expected number of steps until all balls share one color.

Solution

Trace ancestral colors backward. When $k$ ancestral lineages remain among $n$ positions, one update coalesces two lineages with probability $[k/n][(k-1)/(n-1)]=k(k-1)/[n(n-1)]$. The expected time to go from $k$ lineages to $k-1$ is therefore $n(n-1)/[k(k-1)]$. Summing from $k=n$ down to $2$ gives

$$n(n-1)\sum_{k=2}^n\frac1{k(k-1)}=(n-1)^2.$$

Z102 · Drunk man / 醉汉随机游走 （Page 132）

中文: 醉汉站在一座 100 米长桥的第 17 米处，每步以 1/2 概率向前或向后走 1 米。先到达第 100 米而不是第 0 米的概率是多少？到达任一端所需步数的期望是多少？

English: A symmetric random walk starts at meter 17 of a bridge with endpoints 0 and 100. Find the probability it hits 100 first and the expected time to hit either endpoint.

Solution

For an unbiased walk on $\{0,\ldots,100\}$, the probability of hitting $100$ before $0$ from $i$ is $i/100$, hence $17/100$. The expected absorption time solves $e_i=1+(e_{i-1}+e_{i+1})/2$ with zero boundary values, yielding $e_i=i(100-i)$. At $i=17$ this is $17\cdot83=1{,}411$ steps.

Quick Notes

  1. 先把一维随机游走的记号说清楚。 令

    $$X_t=i+\sum_{s=1}^t\xi_s,$$

    其中 $X_0=i$，每一步

    $$\Pr(\xi_s=+1)=p,\qquad \Pr(\xi_s=-1)=q=1-p.$$

    $p=q=1/2$ 称为对称或公平随机游走；$p>q$ 表示向右有正漂移，$p<q$ 表示向左有负漂移。首次到达位置 $a$ 的时间记为

    $$T_a=\inf\{t\ge0:X_t=a\}.$$

    在有界区间 $\{0,1,\ldots,N\}$ 上，走到 $0$ 或 $N$ 就停止，吸收时间为 $T=T_0\wedge T_N$。

  2. Z102 本身的答案。 本题 $N=100$、$i=17$ 且 $p=q=1/2$。公平随机游走先到右端点的概率是当前位置占全长的比例：

    $$\Pr_{17}(T_{100}<T_0)=\frac{17}{100}.$$

    到任一端点的期望步数为

    $$\mathbb E_{17}[T]=17(100-17)=\boxed{1{,}411}.$$

    直觉上，离右端越近，先到右端的概率越高；期望停止时间则在桥中央最大，在端点为零。

  3. 有界区间：先到右端点的概率。 定义

    $$u_i=\Pr_i(T_N<T_0).$$

    边界条件是 $u_0=0,u_N=1$。从内部位置 $i$ 先走一步：

    $$u_i=pu_{i+1}+qu_{i-1}.$$

    解出后得到

    $$\boxed{ u_i= \begin{cases} \dfrac{i}{N},&p=q=\dfrac12,\\[6pt] \dfrac{1-(q/p)^i}{1-(q/p)^N},&p\ne q. \end{cases}}$$

    先到左端点的概率就是 $1-u_i$。当 $p>q$ 时，向右漂移使 $u_i$ 高于公平情形；当 $p<q$ 时则相反。

  4. 原题问的吸收时间：到任一端点的期望步数。 定义

    $$m_i=\mathbb E_i[T_0\wedge T_N].$$

    若已在端点，等待时间为零，所以 $m_0=m_N=0$。从内部位置先花一步，再从新位置继续：

    $$m_i=1+pm_{i+1}+qm_{i-1}.$$

    结果为

    $$\boxed{ m_i= \begin{cases} i(N-i),&p=q=\dfrac12,\\[6pt] \dfrac{Nu_i-i}{p-q},&p\ne q, \end{cases}}$$

    其中 $u_i$ 是上一点的先到右端概率。有限区间且 $0<p<1$ 时最终会被吸收，期望时间也是有限的。

  5. 你真正关心的量：到达某个指定端点需要多久？ 假设指定目标是右端点 $N$。在桥模型中，若先到 $0$ 就已经停止，以后不可能再到 $N$。因此若约定失败路径上 $T_N=\infty$，只要 $0<i<N$ 且 $0<p<1$，就有

    $$\boxed{\mathbb E_i[T_N]=\infty},$$

    因为事件 $T_0<T_N$ 具有正概率。真正有限并能回答“成功到达指定端点的那些路径平均走多久”的量是

    $$c_i^{(N)}=\mathbb E_i[T_N\mid T_N<T_0].$$

    公平随机游走中，

    $$\boxed{c_i^{(N)}=\frac{N^2-i^2}{3}}.$$

    若指定目标是左端点 $0$，则对称地

    $$\boxed{c_i^{(0)}=\mathbb E_i[T_0\mid T_0<T_N] =\frac{N^2-(N-i)^2}{3} =\frac{i(2N-i)}{3}}.$$

    本题从 $17$ 出发：

    $$\mathbb E_{17}[T_{100}\mid T_{100}<T_0] =\frac{100^2-17^2}{3}=3{,}237,$$

    $$\mathbb E_{17}[T_0\mid T_0<T_{100}] =\frac{100^2-83^2}{3}=1{,}037.$$

    它们与原题的吸收时间满足全期望检查：

    $$\frac{17}{100}(3{,}237)+\frac{83}{100}(1{,}037)=1{,}411.$$

    所以“到任一端平均 $1{,}411$ 步”与“给定成功到右端平均 $3{,}237$ 步”是两个不同问题。

  6. 有偏随机游走到指定端点的条件期望。 令 $r=q/p$ 且 $p\ne q$。给定先到右端 $N$，

    $$\boxed{ \mathbb E_i[T_N\mid T_N<T_0] =\frac{1}{p-q}\left[ N\frac{1+r^N}{1-r^N} -i\frac{1+r^i}{1-r^i} \right]}.$$

    若给定先到左端 $0$，交换左右即可。写成显式公式：

    $$\boxed{ \mathbb E_i[T_0\mid T_0<T_N] =\frac{1}{q-p}\left[ N\frac{1+(p/q)^N}{1-(p/q)^N} -(N-i)\frac{1+(p/q)^{N-i}}{1-(p/q)^{N-i}} \right]}.$$

    这两个表达式在 $p\to1/2$ 时分别趋向上一点的 $(N^2-i^2)/3$ 与 $[N^2-(N-i)^2]/3$。即使漂移远离目标，只要条件化在最终先到目标，条件期望仍然有限。

  7. 为什么公平情形的期望时间是二次函数？ 公平递推式可改写为

    $$m_{i+1}-2m_i+m_{i-1}=-2.$$

    左边是离散二阶差分。连续微积分中“二阶导数是常数”会产生二次函数；离散情形完全类似，所以 $m_i$ 应是关于 $i$ 的二次式。再用 $m_0=m_N=0$ 固定两端，便得到 $m_i=i(N-i)$。

  8. 无界情形先要澄清“端点”的意思。 整数直线 $\mathbb Z$ 本身没有端点；通常问题是“是否会首次到达某个固定 level”，或者只保留一个吸收边界，例如在半直线 $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ 上问何时到达 $0$。无界以后，“最终会到达”和“平均多久到达”是两件不同的事。

  9. 半直线 $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$：到达 $0$ 的结论。 从 $i>0$ 出发：

    • 若 $p<q$，漂移指向 $0$：

    $$\Pr_i(T_0<\infty)=1,\qquad \mathbb E_i[T_0]=\frac{i}{q-p}.$$

    • 若 $p=q=1/2$，公平随机游走：

    $$\Pr_i(T_0<\infty)=1,\qquad \boxed{\mathbb E_i[T_0]=\infty}.$$

    • 若 $p>q$，漂移远离 $0$：

    $$\Pr_i(T_0<\infty)=\left(\frac qp\right)^i<1,$$

    所以把“永远不到达”记作 $T_0=\infty$ 时，无条件期望必为 $\infty$。若额外条件化在最终确实到达，则

    $$\mathbb E_i[T_0\mid T_0<\infty]=\frac{i}{p-q}.$$

  10. 无界结论中最反直觉的一点。 公平一维随机游走是 recurrent：它以概率 $1$ 最终会到达任意固定整数；但首次到达时间有很重的尾部，均值是无穷大。因此

    $$\Pr(T_a<\infty)=1$$

    绝不推出 $\mathbb E[T_a]<\infty$。所谓“几乎必然会发生”只说明永远不发生的概率为零，不说明平均等待时间有限。

  11. 无限直线上到达右侧目标的统一版本。 若目标 $a>i$，距离 $d=a-i$，则：

    • $p>q$，漂移朝向目标：到达概率为 $1$，且 $\mathbb E_i[T_a]=d/(p-q)$；
    • $p=q$：到达概率为 $1$，但 $\mathbb E_i[T_a]=\infty$；
    • $p<q$，漂移远离目标：到达概率为 $(p/q)^d<1$，无条件期望为 $\infty$；给定最终到达时，条件期望为 $d/(q-p)$。

    若目标在左侧，只需交换左右，即交换 $p$ 与 $q$。

  12. 怎样由有界结果推出无界结果？ 先在 $[0,N]$ 上求解，再令 $N\to\infty$，比直接在无穷区间上猜边界条件更安全。例如 $p>q$ 时令 $r=q/p<1$：

    $$\Pr_i(T_0<T_N)=1-u_i =\frac{r^i-r^N}{1-r^N}\longrightarrow r^i =\left(\frac qp\right)^i.$$

    当 $p<q$ 时，该极限为 $1$，而有界期望时间公式的极限是 $i/(q-p)$。公平情形下

    $$m_i^{(N)}=i(N-i)\longrightarrow\infty,$$

    这直接显示：虽然最终到达 $0$ 的概率趋于 $1$，期望等待时间却发散。

  13. 鞅可以作为快速交叉检查。 公平随机游走中，$X_t$ 与 $X_t^2-t$ 都是 martingale。对有界吸收时间 $T=T_0\wedge T_N$ 使用停时思想：

    $$\mathbb E_i[X_T]=i.$$

    而 $X_T$ 只可能是 $0$ 或 $N$，所以

    $$i=N\Pr_i(X_T=N),$$

    立即得到 $u_i=i/N$。再由

    $$\mathbb E_i[X_T^2-T]=i^2$$

    以及 $\mathbb E[X_T^2]=N^2u_i=Ni$，得到

    $$\mathbb E_i[T]=Ni-i^2=i(N-i).$$

    在无界问题中不能不检查条件就机械套 optional stopping；公平首次到达时间的期望正是无穷大，很多简单版本的停时定理并不适用。

  14. 最后重点：递推方程怎样一步步解成闭式？

    方法 A：公平情形用一阶差分降阶。 对 hitting probability，

    $$u_{i+1}-2u_i+u_{i-1}=0.$$

    令 $d_i=u_i-u_{i-1}$，则 $d_{i+1}-d_i=0$，所以 $d_i$ 是常数，$u_i=A+Bi$。代入 $u_0=0,u_N=1$ 得 $u_i=i/N$。

    对 expected time，

    $$m_{i+1}-2m_i+m_{i-1}=-2.$$

    仍令 $d_i=m_i-m_{i-1}$，则

    $$d_{i+1}-d_i=-2,$$

    所以 $d_i=d_1-2(i-1)$。累加一阶差分：

    $$m_i=\sum_{k=1}^id_k=id_1-i(i-1).$$

    再用 $m_N=0$ 得 $d_1=N-1$，因此

    $$m_i=i(N-1)-i(i-1)=i(N-i).$$

    方法 B：有偏情形用特征方程。 齐次递推

    $$pu_{i+1}-u_i+qu_{i-1}=0$$

    尝试 $u_i=r^i$，得到

    $$pr^2-r+q=(r-1)(pr-q)=0.$$

    两个根是 $1$ 和 $q/p$，因此

    $$u_i=A+B\left(\frac qp\right)^i.$$

    代入两个边界条件即可解出 $A,B$。期望时间递推的左边相同、右边为 $-1$；先取一个特解 $i/(q-p)$，再加齐次通解：

    $$m_i=A+B\left(\frac qp\right)^i+\frac{i}{q-p},$$

    最后用 $m_0=m_N=0$ 解常数。

    方法 C：指定端点的条件期望先乘回成功概率。 直接对条件期望 $c_i^{(N)}$ 做 first-step analysis 时，条件化会改变每一步的转移概率。更方便的是先定义

    $$g_i=\mathbb E_i\!\left[(T_0\wedge T_N)\mathbf 1_{\{T_N<T_0\}}\right].$$

    因为只在最终成功到达 $N$ 的路径上累计时间，

    $$c_i^{(N)}=\frac{g_i}{u_i}.$$

    先走一步后，若最终成功，这一步也要计入；“该路径最终成功”的概率正是 $u_i$，所以

    $$\boxed{g_i=u_i+pg_{i+1}+qg_{i-1}},\qquad g_0=g_N=0.$$

    公平情形 $u_i=i/N$，于是

    $$g_{i+1}-2g_i+g_{i-1}=-\frac{2i}{N}.$$

    右边关于 $i$ 是一次函数，所以尝试三次特解；结合 $g_0=g_N=0$ 得

    $$g_i=\frac{i(N^2-i^2)}{3N}.$$

    再除以 $u_i=i/N$：

    $$c_i^{(N)}=\frac{g_i}{u_i}=\frac{N^2-i^2}{3}.$$

    有偏情形中 $u_i=A+B(q/p)^i$，$g_i$ 的非齐次项正好由齐次解 $1$ 和 $(q/p)^i$ 组成，因此特解要尝试 $Ci+Di(q/p)^i$；加上齐次通解、代入 $g_0=g_N=0$，再除以 $u_i$，就得到前面的条件期望公式。

    记忆成固定流程：写边界 → 写内部递推 → 齐次解 → 找特解 → 用边界定常数。若问题带有“给定先到指定端点”，先构造 $g_i=\mathbb E[T\mathbf1_{\text{success}}]$，最后除以成功概率；在无界区间只给一个边界条件通常不够，还要用有界截断取极限、最小非负解或合理增长条件排除伪解。

一句话记忆：“到任一端”的吸收时间与“到指定端点”的时间不同：后者在失败路径上无穷，所以通常问条件期望。公平有界游走给定先到 $N$ 时为 $(N^2-i^2)/3$；无限直线上公平游走虽以概率 $1$ 命中固定点，期望命中时间仍是无穷大。

Z106 · Dynamic programming (DP) algorithm / 动态规划算法 （Page 138）

中文: 说明动态规划中的最优性原理，以及如何从终止阶段开始向前逆推价值函数和最优策略。

English: State the principle of optimality and explain the backward recursion used in a finite-horizon dynamic-programming problem.

Solution

The principle of optimality says that the tail of an optimal policy must itself be optimal for the state reached. For terminal cost $g_N$, set $J_N(x)=g_N(x)$ and recurse backward:

$$J_k(x)=\min_u E\!\left[g_k(x,u,W_k)+J_{k+1}\!\left(f(x,u,W_k)\right)\right].$$

An optimizer at each state supplies the policy, and $J_0$ gives the optimal expected cost.

Solution

Let $v(r,b)$ be the maximum additional expected payoff with $r$ red and $b$ black cards remaining, measured from the current accumulated payoff. The Bellman recursion is

$$v(r,b)=\max\left\{0,\frac{r[1+v(r-1,b)]+b[-1+v(r,b-1)]}{r+b}\right\},$$

with $v(0,b)=0$ and $v(r,0)=r$. Continue exactly when the continuation expression is positive; otherwise stop.

Z114 · Price direction of options / 期权价格方向 （Page 153）

中文: 当标的价格 $S$、执行价 $K$、剩余期限、波动率 $\sigma$、无风险利率 $r$ 或股息发生变化时，普通欧式与美式期权的价格方向分别怎样变化？

Solution

Holding other inputs fixed: increasing $S$ raises calls and lowers puts; increasing $K$ lowers calls and raises puts; increasing $\sigma$ raises both calls and puts. For a non-dividend stock, increasing $r$ raises calls and lowers puts. Increasing dividends lowers calls and raises puts.

Z115 · Put-call parity / 看涨看跌平价 （Page 154）

English: State and prove put–call parity for European options on a non-dividend-paying stock.

Solution

For strike $K$ and maturity $T$,

$$C+Ke^{-rT}=P+S_0.$$

Portfolio 1 is a call plus a zero-coupon bond paying $K$; portfolio 2 is a put plus one share. At $T$ both pay $\max(S_T,K)$. Equal terminal cash flows imply equal current prices by no arbitrage.

Z116 · American v.s. European options / 美式与欧式期权 （Page 155）

中文: A：无股息股票上的美式看涨期权为什么不应提前行权？

English: A. Why is early exercise of an American call on a non-dividend-paying stock never optimal?

B. European puts on the same non-dividend-paying stock and maturity have prices $P(80)=8$ and $P(90)=9$. Is there an arbitrage?

Solution

A. Before expiry, $C_A\ge C_E\ge S_0-Ke^{-r\tau}>S_0-K$ when $r>0$. Exercising gives only $S_0-K$, sacrifices remaining optionality, and pays $K$ earlier than necessary. With zero rates, waiting is still weakly better. Thus early exercise is not optimal and the American and European call values coincide.

B. The put spread costs $9-8=1$ and pays between $0$ and $10$, so no arbitrage requires $0\le P(90)-P(80)\le10e^{-rT}$. The quotes are consistent when $1\le10e^{-rT}$, as under ordinary rate and maturity inputs; if that inequality fails, the spread is overpriced and can be arbitraged. Without $r$ and $T$, an unconditional yes/no conclusion is not available.

Z117 · Black-Scholes-Merton differential equation / BSM 偏微分方程 （Page 158）

中文: 写出 Black–Scholes–Merton 偏微分方程，并简要说明其推导思路。

English: State the Black–Scholes–Merton PDE and briefly derive it by delta hedging.

Solution

For an asset with continuous dividend yield $q$, the PDE is

$$V_t+\frac12\sigma^2S^2V_{SS}+(r-q)SV_S-rV=0.$$

Form a portfolio long one derivative and short $\Delta=V_S$ shares. Itô's lemma shows the diffusion terms cancel. The locally riskless portfolio must earn $r$; equating its drift to the risk-free return and accounting for dividends yields the PDE. The terminal payoff and boundary conditions select the particular derivative price.

Z118 · Black-Scholes formula / Black-Scholes 公式 （Page 160）

中文: A：Black–Scholes 公式依赖哪些假设？

B：用风险中性定价推导无股息股票的欧式看涨期权公式。

C：从 Black–Scholes–Merton 偏微分方程推导同一公式。

D：零利率、无股息且 $S_0=1$；一项无到期日合约在股价首次触及 $H>1$ 时支付 $1$。求当前价值。

E：无股息股票服从几何布朗运动；一项合约在 $T$ 时支付 $1/S_T$。求其价值。

English: A. State the assumptions behind the Black–Scholes formula.

B. Derive the non-dividend-paying European call price under the risk-neutral measure.

C. Derive the same formula from the Black–Scholes–Merton PDE.

D. With zero rates, no dividends, $S_0=1$, and no expiry, a claim pays $1$ when $S$ first hits $H>1$. Find its value.

E. For a non-dividend-paying geometric Brownian stock, price a claim paying $1/S_T$ at maturity $T$.

Solution

A. The standard model assumes frictionless continuous trading; no arbitrage; unrestricted borrowing/shorting; constant known $r$, dividend yield $q$, and volatility $\sigma$; and a lognormal stock with continuous paths $dS=(\mu-q)S\,dt+\sigma S\,dW$. European exercise and known terminal payoff are also assumed.

B. Under $Q$, $S_T=S_0\exp[(r-\sigma^2/2)T+\sigma\sqrt T\,Z]$. Discounting $E^Q[(S_T-K)^+]$ and integrating over the normal tail gives

$$C=S_0N(d_1)-Ke^{-rT}N(d_2),$$

where $d_1=[\ln(S_0/K)+(r+\sigma^2/2)T]/(\sigma\sqrt T)$ and $d_2=d_1-\sigma\sqrt T$.

C. Solve $V_t+(1/2)\sigma^2S^2V_{SS}+rSV_S-rV=0$ with $V(S,T)=(S-K)^+$. The substitutions $x=\ln(S/K)$, time-to-expiry $\tau$, and an exponential rescaling remove the first-derivative and discount terms, reducing the PDE to the heat equation. Convolution with the Gaussian heat kernel and undoing the substitutions gives the same formula in part B.

D. With $r=0$, $S$ is a nonnegative martingale. Stop at the first hit of $H$ and localize with a lower barrier $\varepsilon>0$. If $p_\varepsilon$ is the probability of hitting $H$ before $\varepsilon$, optional stopping gives $1=p_\varepsilon H+(1-p_\varepsilon)\varepsilon$. Letting $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ yields $P(\tau_H<\infty)=1/H$, which is also the claim value.

E. Under $Q$, $E[1/S_T]=(1/S_0)e^{(-r+\sigma^2)T}$. Discounting once more gives $V_0=(1/S_0)e^{(\sigma^2-2r)T}$.

Z128 · Value at risk / 风险价值 （Page 180）

中文: 简要解释风险价值（VaR），并说明用 VaR 衡量衍生品风险可能有哪些缺陷。

English: Briefly define value at risk (VaR) and explain drawbacks of using it for derivative portfolios.

Solution

At confidence $\alpha$ and horizon $h$, VaR is a loss quantile: the smallest $l$ such that $P(\mathrm{Loss}\le l)\ge\alpha$. It says how large a loss is not exceeded with the chosen confidence under the model.

VaR does not describe loss severity beyond the quantile, can fail subadditivity, and can be unstable for nonlinear portfolios with jumps, sparse data, or model error. It also depends strongly on horizon, revaluation method, and distributional assumptions. Expected shortfall and stress tests address some of these gaps.

Z129 · Duration and convexity / 久期与凸性 （Page 182）

中文: 一只面值 100 美元、年票息率为 $30\%-3r$、5 年到期且每半年付息的反向浮息债券，在当前收益率曲线平坦且为 7.5% 时，价格和久期分别是多少？

English: An inverse floater has face value USD 100, annual coupon rate $30\%-3r$, semiannual coupons, and five years to maturity. With a flat 7.5% yield curve, find its price and duration.

Solution

Replicate the inverse floater by going long four par fixed-rate bonds paying 7.5% and short three par floating-rate notes. Its coupon is $4(7.5\%)-3r=30\%-3r$ and its principal is $4\cdot100-3\cdot100=100$. Both components are at par on the flat 7.5% curve, so the price is USD 100.

Let $y=0.075$ and $m=10$ half-year periods. For one fixed-rate bond, differentiating

$$P_{\mathrm{fixed}}(y)=\sum_{k=1}^{10}\frac{3.75}{(1+y/2)^k}+\frac{100}{(1+y/2)^{10}}$$

gives dollar duration $-dP_{\mathrm{fixed}}/dy\approx410.64$. A par floating-rate note just before its next coupon has value $103.75$ payable over the next half-year for duration purposes, so its dollar duration is $0.5(103.75)/(1+y/2)^2\approx48.19$. Hence the inverse-floater dollar duration is $4(410.64)-3(48.19)\approx1{,}498$, and its duration is $1{,}498/100\approx14.98$ years. The duration can exceed the five-year maturity because the inverse coupon embeds leveraged negative exposure to the floating rate.

Z130 · Forward and futures / 远期与期货 （Page 183）

中文: 远期合约与期货合约有什么区别？若标的资产价格与随机利率高度正相关，期货价和远期价哪个更高？为什么？

English: Contrast forwards and futures. With stochastic interest rates and strong positive correlation between the underlying price and rates, which price is higher, and why?

Solution

A forward is an OTC contract settled mainly at maturity and can be customized; a futures contract is standardized, exchange-traded, margined, and marked to market daily. With stochastic rates and positive price–rate correlation, futures gains tend to arrive when rates are high and can be reinvested advantageously, while losses tend to occur when financing is cheaper. Consequently the futures price is generally higher than the otherwise comparable forward price.

Z131 · Interest rate models / 利率模型 （Page 184）

中文: 介绍几种基本利率模型，并比较它们的主要差异。

English: Explain several basic short-rate models and compare their main features.

Solution

Ho–Lee uses $dr=\theta(t)\,dt+\sigma\,dW$ and fits the initial curve but has no mean reversion and permits negative rates. Vasicek adds Gaussian mean reversion, $dr=\kappa(\theta-r)\,dt+\sigma\,dW$, but still permits negative rates. CIR uses $dr=\kappa(\theta-r)\,dt+\sigma\sqrt r\,dW$, making volatility state-dependent and keeping rates nonnegative under the Feller condition. Hull–White makes the mean level time-dependent so a mean-reverting Gaussian model fits today's curve exactly. The trade-offs are curve fit, mean reversion, positivity, tractability, and the volatility structure.

Z132 · Number swap / 数字交换 （Page 188）

中文: 不使用额外存储空间，怎样交换两个整数 $i$ 和 $j$ 的值？

English: Swap two integers $i$ and $j$ without additional storage.

Solution

For distinct storage locations, XOR swap works: i ^= j; j ^= i; i ^= j. Arithmetic i = i + j; j = i - j; i = i - j is another option but may overflow. In production, an ordinary temporary variable is clearer and usually optimized just as well; XOR swap also fails if the two references alias the same location.

Z133 · Unique elements / 唯一元素 （Page 189）

中文: 给定一个已排序数组，编写算法提取其中不重复的元素。例如 $[1,1,3,3,3,5,5,5,9,9,9,9]$ 应得到 $[1,3,5,9]$。

English: Given a sorted array, output its unique elements; for example $[1,1,3,3,3,5,5,5,9,9,9,9]$ becomes $[1,3,5,9]$.

Solution

Scan once with a write index. Keep the first element; for each later element, write it only if it differs from the last retained value. This takes $O(n)$ time and $O(1)$ extra space when compacting in place, and the retained prefix is the unique array.

Z134 · Horner's algorithm / Horner 算法 （Page 190）

中文: 用 Horner 算法计算多项式 $y=A_0+A_1x+A_2x^2+\cdots+A_nx^n$。

English: Give an efficient algorithm to evaluate $A_0+A_1x+\cdots+A_nx^n$.

Solution

Use Horner's rule: set $y=A_n$ and for $k=n-1,\ldots,0$ update $y=y\,x+A_k$. The result is $A_0+x(A_1+x(A_2+\cdots+xA_n))$. It uses $n$ multiplications, $n$ additions, $O(n)$ time, and $O(1)$ space.

Z135 · Moving average / 移动平均 （Page 190）

中文: 给定长度为 $m$ 的大数组 $A$，设计高效算法计算窗口长度为 $n$ 的移动平均数组。

English: For an array of length $m$, compute every $n$-element moving average efficiently.

Solution

Compute the first window sum in $O(n)$. When the window moves one place, update sum += new_element - departing_element and divide by $n$. All $m-n+1$ averages are produced in $O(m)$ time and $O(1)$ auxiliary space besides the output.

Z136 · Sorting algorithm / 排序算法 （Page 190）

中文: 说明三种对 $n$ 个互不相同的值进行排序的算法，并分析各自的时间复杂度。

English: Explain three algorithms for sorting $n$ distinct values and analyze their time complexity.

Solution

Insertion sort inserts each item into a sorted prefix: $O(n^2)$ worst/average time, $O(n)$ best on sorted input, and $O(1)$ space. Merge sort recursively sorts halves and merges: $O(n\log n)$ time in all cases and $O(n)$ auxiliary space in the usual array implementation. Quicksort partitions around pivots: expected $O(n\log n)$, worst $O(n^2)$, with expected $O(\log n)$ stack space; random pivots make the bad case unlikely.

Z137 · Random permutation / 随机排列 （Page 192）

中文: A：已有均匀随机数生成器，怎样洗一副 52 张牌，使每种排列等概率？

B：一个文件只能顺序读取，长度未知。怎样选出一个字符，使每个字符被选中的概率相同？

English: A. Using uniform random numbers, shuffle 52 cards so every permutation is equally likely.

B. A file can be read only sequentially and its length is unknown. Select one character so every character has equal probability of being chosen.

Solution

A. Use Fisher–Yates. For $i=51$ down to $1$, choose $j$ uniformly from $\{0,\ldots,i\}$ and swap cards $i$ and $j$. Every permutation has probability $\prod_{i=1}^{51}1/(i+1)=1/52!$.

B. Use reservoir sampling. Keep the first character. When the $k$-th character arrives, replace the stored character with probability $1/k$. Induction shows each of the $k$ characters is retained with probability $1/k$; no file length is needed.

Z138 · Search algorithm / 搜索算法 （Page 193）

中文: A：用不超过 $3n/2$ 次比较，同时找出 $n$ 个数的最小值和最大值。

B：一个长度未知的数组先全为 0，从某处起全为非零；在不知道数组大小的情况下找出第一个非零位置。

C：一个 $n\times n$ 矩阵每行从左到右递增、每列从上到下递增。设计查找给定数的算法，并分析复杂度。

English: A. Find both minimum and maximum among $n$ numbers with at most $3n/2$ comparisons.

B. An array has an unknown-length prefix of zeros followed only by nonzeros, and its size is unknown. Find the first nonzero position.

C. Rows and columns of an $n\times n$ matrix increase. Find a target and analyze complexity.

Solution

A. Pair the numbers. Compare within each pair, send the smaller to a minimum tournament and the larger to a maximum tournament. For even $n$ this uses $n/2+2(n/2-1)=3n/2-2$ comparisons, within the bound.

B. Probe indices $1,2,4,8,\ldots$ until finding a nonzero value, then binary-search between the last zero index and that index. This takes $O(\log k)$ probes when the first nonzero is at $k$.

C. Start at the top-right corner. If the entry is too large move left; if too small move down; if equal stop. Each step eliminates a row or column, so an $n\times n$ matrix takes $O(n)$ comparisons.

Z139 · Fibonacci numbers / 斐波那契数 （Page 195）

中文: 给出的递归程序用 $F(n-1)+F(n-2)$ 计算斐波那契数。若计算 $F(n)$ 需要 $100$ 秒，计算 $F(n+1)$ 约需多少秒？该算法是否高效？还能怎样计算斐波那契数？

English: A naive recursive Fibonacci program computes $F(n)$ in $100$ seconds for large $n$. Estimate the time for $F(n+1)$ to the nearest second. Is the algorithm efficient, and how should Fibonacci numbers be computed?

Solution

The naive runtime obeys essentially the Fibonacci recurrence, so $T(n+1)/T(n)$ tends to the golden ratio $\varphi\approx1.618$. The estimate is 162 seconds. The recursion is exponential and repeats subproblems. An iterative two-variable method is $O(n)$ time and $O(1)$ space; matrix exponentiation or fast doubling computes $F(n)$ in $O(\log n)$ arithmetic operations.

Z140 · Maximum contiguous subarray / 最大连续子数组 （Page 196）

中文: 一维数组 $A$ 长度为 $n$，元素有正有负。设计算法找出和最大的连续子数组。

English: Design an algorithm to find a maximum-sum contiguous subarray of an array containing positive and negative values.

Solution

Kadane's algorithm maintains $\mathrm{best\_ending\_here}=\max(A_i,\mathrm{best\_ending\_here}+A_i)$ and $\mathrm{best\_so\_far}=\max(\mathrm{best\_so\_far},\mathrm{best\_ending\_here})$, recording start/end indices when the first term is chosen or a new maximum appears. Initialize from $A_1$ so all-negative arrays are handled. Time is $O(n)$ and extra space $O(1)$.

Z141 · Power of 2? / 是否为 2 的幂 （Page 198）

中文: 怎样判断一个整数是否为 2 的幂？

English: Determine whether an integer is a power of two.

Solution

For a positive integer $x$, $x$ is a power of two exactly when x & (x - 1) equals zero: a power of two has one set bit, and subtracting one turns that bit off and all lower bits on. Therefore test x > 0 && (x & (x - 1)) == 0.

Z142 · Multiplication by 7 / 乘以 7 （Page 198）

中文: 不使用乘法运算符，怎样快速把一个整数乘以 7？

English: Multiply an integer by 7 quickly without using the multiplication operator.

Solution

Use $7x=8x-x$, implemented as (x << 3) - x. For fixed-width signed integers, ensure the shift and subtraction do not overflow and follow the language's rules for negative shifts.

Z143 · Probability simulation / 概率模拟 （Page 198）

中文: 只有一枚公平硬币时，怎样设计一个简单随机试验，使获胜概率恰好为任意给定的 $p$（$0<p<1$）？

English: Using only a fair coin, construct a game whose winning probability is any prescribed $p$ with $0<p<1$.

Solution

Write $p=0.b_1b_2\ldots$ in binary. Toss the fair coin to generate independent bits $U_1,U_2,\ldots$ of $U\sim U(0,1)$. Compare $U$'s bits with $p$'s bits until the first difference; declare a win if $U<p$. Equality forever has probability zero, so the algorithm terminates almost surely and $P(\text{win})=P(U<p)=p$. Choose a consistent non-terminating binary representation at dyadic $p$.

Z144 · Poisonous wine / 毒酒定位 （Page 199）

中文: 1000 瓶酒中恰有 1 瓶有毒。距聚会还有 20 小时；手上有 10 只实验鼠，老鼠喝到任意剂量毒酒都会在恰好 18 小时后死亡，之前无症状。怎样保证在聚会前找出毒酒？

English: Exactly one of 1,000 wine bottles is poisoned. Ten mice die exactly 18 hours after any exposure, and the party is 20 hours away. Identify the bottle with certainty in one testing round.

Solution

Number bottles 0 through 999 and write each number as a $10$-bit binary string. Mouse $k$ drinks a sample from every bottle whose $k$-th bit is $1$. After $18$ hours, interpret dead mice as $1$ and survivors as $0$. The resulting $10$-bit pattern is the poisoned bottle's number. Since $2^{10}=1{,}024$, ten mice cover all 1,000 bottles in one round.

Z145 · Monte Carlo simulation / 蒙特卡洛模拟 （Page 200）

中文: A：说明如何用蒙特卡洛模拟为欧式看涨期权定价。

B：电脑只能生成 $U(0,1)$ 随机数时，怎样生成 $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$ 随机数？

C：说明几种蒙特卡洛方差缩减方法。

D：没有闭式定价公式时，怎样估计期权的 Delta 和 Gamma？

E：怎样用蒙特卡洛模拟估计 $\pi$？

English: A. Explain Monte Carlo pricing of a European call.

B. Generate $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$ variables using only $U(0,1)$ variables.

C. Explain several Monte Carlo variance-reduction methods.

D. Without a closed-form price, estimate an option's delta and gamma.

E. Estimate $\pi$ by Monte Carlo.

Solution

A. Simulate $Z\sim N(0,1)$, set $S_T=S_0\exp[(r-q-\sigma^2/2)T+\sigma\sqrt T\,Z]$, compute $e^{-rT}(S_T-K)^+$, and average over many independent paths. Report a Monte Carlo standard error as the sample standard deviation divided by $\sqrt N$.

B. Box–Muller: from independent $U_1,U_2\sim U(0,1)$, set

$$Z_1=\sqrt{-2\ln U_1}\cos(2\pi U_2),\qquad Z_2=\sqrt{-2\ln U_1}\sin(2\pi U_2).$$

Then $\mu+\sigma Z_i\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$.

C. Useful methods include antithetic variates, control variates with known expectations, stratified or Latin-hypercube sampling, importance sampling for rare events, conditional Monte Carlo, and quasi-random sequences. Each should preserve unbiasedness or make any bias explicit.

D. Revalue at $S_0+h$, $S_0$, and $S_0-h$ using the same random numbers. Estimate

$$\Delta\approx\frac{V(S_0+h)-V(S_0-h)}{2h},\qquad \Gamma\approx\frac{V(S_0+h)-2V(S_0)+V(S_0-h)}{h^2}.$$

Common random numbers greatly reduce difference-estimator noise; pathwise or likelihood-ratio estimators may be better when applicable.

E. Draw IID points $(X,Y)$ uniformly in $[0,1]^2$. The fraction with $X^2+Y^2\le1$ converges to the quarter-circle area $\pi/4$, so estimate $\pi$ by four times that fraction.

Z146 · Finite difference method / 有限差分法 （Page 205）

中文: A：简要说明用有限差分法求解衍生品定价偏微分方程的基本思路。

B：用显式有限差分法求解抛物型偏微分方程时，时间方向步数过多和空间方向步数过多，哪一种更危险？为什么？

English: A. Briefly explain finite-difference methods for derivative-pricing PDEs.

B. For an explicit scheme for a parabolic PDE, is excessive refinement in time or in space more dangerous, and why?

Solution

A. Lay down a grid in asset state and time, replace PDE derivatives by finite differences, impose terminal and boundary conditions, and step backward to today. Explicit schemes compute the next layer directly; implicit schemes solve a linear system and are more stable; Crank–Nicolson averages the two and is second-order in time for smooth solutions. American exercise is handled by applying the early-exercise constraint at each step.

B. For $u_t=\kappa u_{xx}$, the explicit centered scheme requires $\kappa\Delta t/(\Delta x)^2\le1/2$. More time steps reduce $\Delta t$ and improve stability, though they cost computation. More space steps reduce $\Delta x$; if $\Delta t$ is unchanged, the ratio grows and can make the method unstable. Therefore excessive spatial refinement is the dangerous one unless time is refined proportionally to $(\Delta x)^2$.
