A. Foundations, Games & Decisions

M001 · Problem 1 The Sock Drawer / 袜子抽屉 （Page 1）

中文: 一个抽屉里装有红袜和黑袜。随机不放回抽出两只，两只都是红袜的概率恰好为 $1/2$。（a）抽屉里最少共有多少只袜子？（b）如果黑袜的数量还必须是偶数，最少共有多少只？

English: A drawer contains red and black socks. Two socks are drawn uniformly without replacement, and the probability that both are red is exactly $1/2$. (a) What is the smallest possible total number of socks? (b) What is the smallest total if the number of black socks must also be even?

Solution

Let $r$ and $b$ be the red and black counts and put $N=r+b$. The condition is

$$\frac{\binom r2}{\binom N2}=\frac12,\qquad 2r(r-1)=N(N-1).$$

Checking positive integer solutions in increasing $N$ gives $(r,b)=(3,1)$ first, so part (a) is $4$. The next nontrivial solution is $(r,b)=(15,6)$; it is the first with even $b$, so part (b) is $21$. Direct substitution gives $\binom{15}{2}/\binom{21}{2}=105/210=1/2$.

M002 · Problem 2 Successive Wins / 连赢两局 （Page 1）

中文: Elmer 要打三场网球，只要连续赢两场就能获奖。他可以选择“父亲—冠军—父亲”或“冠军—父亲—冠军”的顺序；父亲比冠军更容易战胜。应选择哪一种顺序？

English: Elmer plays three matches and wins a prize if he wins two consecutive matches. He may play Father–Champion–Father or Champion–Father–Champion, and the champion is the stronger opponent. Which order should he choose?

Solution

Let $p$ be Elmer's chance of beating his father and $q$ his chance of beating the champion, with $p>q$. The middle match must be won. Thus

$$P(F\!-\!C\!-\!F)=q\bigl[1-(1-p)^2\bigr]=pq(2-p),$$

$$P(C\!-\!F\!-\!C)=p\bigl[1-(1-q)^2\bigr]=pq(2-q).$$

Because $p>q$, we have $2-q>2-p$. Elmer should therefore choose Champion–Father–Champion: the easier match belongs in the indispensable middle position.

M003 · Problem 3 The Flippant Juror / 随机裁决的陪审员 （Page 1）

中文: 三人陪审团中，两名陪审员各自独立地以概率 $p$ 作出正确判断，第三名陪审员则抛公平硬币决定。多数票为最终裁决。它是否比一名以概率 $p$ 判断正确的单人陪审员更可靠？

English: In a three-person jury, two jurors independently reach the correct decision with probability $p$, while the third decides by a fair coin toss. Majority rules. Is this jury more reliable than a single juror who is correct with probability $p$?

Solution

The jury is correct when both informed jurors are correct, or when they disagree and the random juror breaks the tie correctly:

$$p^2+2p(1-p)\frac12=p.$$

Its accuracy is exactly the same as that of one informed juror. The random third vote adds no information.

M004 · Problem 4 Trials until First Success / 首次成功前的试验次数 （Page 1）

中文: 反复掷一枚公平六面骰，直到第一次出现 6。平均需要掷多少次？

English: A fair six-sided die is rolled repeatedly until the first six appears. What is the expected number of rolls?

Solution

The waiting time $T$ is geometric with success probability $1/6$:

$$P(T=k)=\left(\frac56\right)^{k-1}\frac16,\qquad \mathbb E[T]=\frac{1}{1/6}=6.$$

Equivalently, after the first roll the process either stops or starts afresh, so $E=1+(5/6)E$, which also gives $E=6$.

M005 · Problem 5 Coin in Square / 方格中的硬币 （Page 2）

中文: 桌面画有边长 1 英寸的正方形网格。把一枚直径 $3/4$ 英寸的硬币抛到桌上；若硬币完整落在某一个方格内就获胜。已知硬币落在桌面上，获胜概率是多少？

English: A table is ruled into one-inch squares. A coin of diameter $3/4$ inch is tossed onto it; the player wins if the coin lies entirely inside one square. Conditional on landing on the table, what is the win probability?

Solution

The coin's center must be at least its radius, $3/8$ inch, from every grid line. Inside a unit cell the allowable center region is therefore a square of side

$$1-2\left(\frac38\right)=\frac14.$$

Uniformity modulo the grid gives

$$P(\text{win})=\left(\frac14\right)^2=\boxed{\frac1{16}}.$$

M006 · Problem 6 Chuck-a-Luck / 三骰押点游戏 （Page 2）

中文: 玩家押注一个点数后掷三枚公平骰子。若所押点数出现 $1$、$2$ 或 $3$ 次，玩家分别净赢 $1$、$2$ 或 $3$ 个单位；若一次也没出现则净输 1 个单位。每押 1 单位的期望损失是多少？

English: A player chooses one face and rolls three fair dice. If that face appears once, twice, or three times, the net profit is respectively one, two, or three units; if it never appears, the player loses one unit. What is the expected loss per unit stake?

Solution

Let $K\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(3,1/6)$ count matches. The payoff equals $K$ when $K>0$ and $-1$ when $K=0$. Hence

$$\mathbb E[\Pi]=\mathbb E[K]-P(K=0)=\frac12-\left(\frac56\right)^3=-\frac{17}{216}.$$

The expected loss is therefore $17/216\approx0.0787$ unit, a house edge of about $7.87\%$.

M007 · Problem 7 Curing the Compulsive Gambler / 治疗强迫性赌徒 （Page 2）

中文: Brown 每次在美式轮盘上用 1 美元押数字 13，共玩 36 次。若押中，净赢 35 美元；否则输掉 1 美元。他的朋友另与他以 20 美元作等额对赌，赌 Brown 在 36 次结束后会亏钱。这个“治疗方案”是怎样运作的？

English: Brown stakes one dollar on number 13 in each of 36 plays of American roulette. A hit earns a net \$35 and a miss loses \$1. His friend also makes an even-money \$20 side bet that Brown will be behind after the 36 plays. How does this attempted cure work?

Solution

One hit makes Brown exactly even with the casino after 36 plays, so he is behind only if all 36 bets lose:

$$P(\text{behind})=\left(\frac{37}{38}\right)^{36}\approx0.3830.$$

Brown's expected roulette loss is $36(2/38)=36/19\approx1.8947$ dollars. Because the friend pays Brown when Brown is not behind, Brown's expected side-bet profit is

$$20\bigl[1-2(37/38)^{36}\bigr]\approx4.681.$$

The combined expectation is about \$2.79 for Brown. The joke is that the friend, not Brown, may be cured first; however, if all roulette bets lose, Brown loses \$36+\$20=\$56, a deliberately painful outcome.

M008 · Problem 8 Perfect Bridge Hand / 完美桥牌手牌 （Page 2）

中文: 标准 52 张扑克牌有四种花色，每种 13 张。桥牌每位玩家发 13 张牌。某位指定玩家拿到 13 张同一花色牌的概率是多少？

English: A standard 52-card deck has four suits of 13 cards, and a bridge player is dealt 13 cards. What is the probability that a specified player receives all 13 cards of one suit?

Solution

There are $\binom{52}{13}$ equally likely hands. Exactly four of them are single-suit hands, one for each suit. Therefore

$$P=\frac{4}{\binom{52}{13}}=\frac{4\,13!\,39!}{52!}\approx6.30\times10^{-12}.$$

This is about one chance in $1.59\times10^{11}$ for a specified player.

M009 · Problem 9 Craps / Craps 的获胜概率 （Page 2）

中文: Craps 的规则如下：首次掷两骰，点数和为 7 或 11 立即获胜，为 2、3 或 12 立即失败；其他点数和成为 point，之后若先再次掷出 point 则胜，若先掷出 7 则负。玩家最终获胜的概率是多少？

English: In craps, a first-roll total of 7 or 11 wins immediately, while 2, 3, or 12 loses. Any other total becomes the point; the player then wins by rolling that point again before rolling 7. What is the overall win probability?

Solution

The immediate-win probability is $8/36$. For points $4,5,6,8,9,10$, the eventual win probabilities are respectively $3/9,4/10,5/11,5/11,4/10,3/9$. Thus

$$P=\frac8{36}+2\left(\frac3{36}\frac39+\frac4{36}\frac4{10}+\frac5{36}\frac5{11}\right)=\boxed{\frac{244}{495}}\approx0.49293.$$

The house advantage on an even-money pass-line bet is about $1-2P\approx1.414\%$.

M010 · Problem 10 An Experiment in Personal Taste for Money / 对金钱偏好的实验 （Page 3）

中文: （a）一个罐中有 10 个黑球和 10 个白球。你先猜颜色，随机抽一球；猜中得 10 美元，否则得 0。你最多愿意付多少钱玩一次？（b）朋友可自行决定放入多少黑球和白球，但你仍可先选颜色。此时最多愿意付多少？

English: (a) An urn contains ten black and ten white balls. You choose a color, draw once, and receive \$10 for a match and \$0 otherwise. What is the most you would pay to play once? (b) A friend chooses the black-white composition to suit himself, after which you choose a color. What would you pay now?

Solution

There is no universal monetary answer without a utility function: a risk-averse player may pay less than expected value. Under risk-neutral valuation, part (a) is worth

$$10\cdot\frac12=\$5.$$

For part (b), randomize the color choice with a fair coin. Whatever composition the friend selects, the unconditional match probability is $1/2$, so the guaranteed expected payoff is again \$5. If the player has information about the friend's choice, it may be possible to do better. Thus the risk-neutral guaranteed value is at least \$5, while the personal maximum depends on utility and available information.

M011 · Problem 11 Silent Cooperation / 无声合作 （Page 3）

中文: 两名互不相识的人分别从所有正整数中选择一个数；若两人选中同一个数，双方都获奖。不能沟通时，你会选哪个数？

English: Two strangers separately choose one positive integer. If they choose the same integer, both receive a prize. Without communication, which number should you choose?

Solution

This is a coordination problem without a specified probability distribution, so mathematics does not determine a unique optimum. The rational goal is to choose a focal point: the number most likely to seem salient to the other player. The natural choice is $1$, because it is the first and simplest positive integer. Other culturally salient choices could be defensible, but they rely on shared conventions rather than probability alone.

M012 · Problem 12 Quo Vadis? / 该去哪里会合？ （Page 4）

中文: 两名陌生人约定星期四中午在纽约会面，却忘了确定具体地点；两人都无法再联系对方，也都不熟悉纽约。若仍想尽量碰面，应去哪里？

English: Two strangers agree to meet at noon on Thursday in New York City but neglect to choose a location. Neither can contact the other, and neither knows the city well. Where should each go to maximize the chance of meeting?

Solution

Again there is no probability model and hence no theorem selecting one location. They should independently choose the most prominent, compact, and mutually salient landmark. In the book's setting, the Empire State Building is a sensible focal point: it is famous, central, and more spatially precise than a broad area such as Times Square. The important concept is common salience, not a calculable numerical optimum.

M013 · Problem 13 The Prisoner’s Dilemma / 囚犯获释信息问题 （Page 4）

中文: 三名囚犯 A、B、C 中恰有两人会获释，各种二人组合先验等可能。A 请守卫说出 B、C 中一名确定会获释的人。A 认为听到“B 会获释”后，自己的获释概率会从 $2/3$ 降到 $1/2$，所以不敢问。错在哪里？

English: Exactly two of prisoners A, B, and C will be released, with the three possible pairs initially equally likely. A asks the guard to name one of B or C who will be released. A fears that hearing “B will be released” would reduce his own chance from $2/3$ to $1/2$. What is wrong?

Solution

The guard's reporting rule matters. Under the natural rule that he chooses B or C with equal probability when both are released,

$$P(AB\text{ and says B})=\frac13,\qquad P(BC\text{ and says B})=\frac13\cdot\frac12=\frac16.$$

Therefore

$$P(A\text{ released}\mid\text{guard says B})=\frac{1/3}{1/3+1/6}=\frac23.$$

The two remaining scenarios are not equally likely after conditioning. Asking cannot change the already chosen release pair; with the symmetric protocol, A's probability remains $2/3$. A different guard protocol could change the posterior, which is why it must be stated.

M014 · Problem 14 Collecting Coupons / 收集优惠券 （Page 4）

中文: 每盒麦片独立且等概率地附带编号 1 至 5 中的一张优惠券。集齐五种优惠券平均需要买多少盒？并给出集齐 $m$ 种等概率优惠券时的近似。

English: Each cereal box independently contains one of five equally likely coupon types. What is the expected number of boxes needed to collect all five? Also give the approximation for $m$ equally likely types.

Solution

After $k$ types have been collected, a new type appears with probability $(5-k)/5$, so the expected additional wait is $5/(5-k)$. Hence

$$\mathbb E[T]=5H_5=5\left(1+\frac12+\frac13+\frac14+\frac15\right)=\boxed{\frac{137}{12}}\approx11.4167.$$

For $m$ types, $\mathbb E[T]=mH_m$. Euler's expansion gives

$$mH_m=m\log m+\gamma m+\frac12-\frac{1}{12m}+O(m^{-3}),$$

where $\gamma$ is the Euler–Mascheroni constant.

B. Counting, Sampling & Strategic Probability

M015 · Problem 15 The Theater Row / 剧院的一排座位 （Page 4）

中文: 8 名单身男子与 7 名女模特各自随机买到剧院同一排 15 个连续座位中的一个。平均会有多少对相邻座位恰好是一男一女？

English: Eight bachelors and seven models independently receive the 15 seats in one theater row in a uniformly random order. What is the expected number of adjacent bachelor-model pairs?

Solution

There are $14$ adjacent seat boundaries. For any one boundary, the probability of unlike occupants is

$$\frac8{15}\frac7{14}+\frac7{15}\frac8{14}=\frac8{15}.$$

Let $I_j$ indicate an unlike pair at boundary $j$. By linearity of expectation, without needing independence,

$$\mathbb E\left[\sum_{j=1}^{14}I_j\right]=14\cdot\frac8{15}=\boxed{\frac{112}{15}}\approx7.467.$$

M016 · Problem 16 Will Second-Best Be Runner-Up? / 第二名会成为亚军吗？ （Page 4）

中文: 8 名选手随机进入固定的单败淘汰赛签表。最强者一定战胜所有人，第二强者一定战胜其余六人。第二强者最终获得亚军的概率是多少？

English: Eight players are placed uniformly into a fixed single-elimination bracket. The best always beats everyone, and the second-best beats the other six. What is the probability that the second-best finishes runner-up?

Solution

The second-best reaches the final exactly when the best player is in the opposite half of the bracket. Once the best player's slot is fixed, four of the seven remaining slots lie in the opposite half. Therefore

$$P(\text{runner-up})=\boxed{\frac47}.$$

M017 · Problem 17 Twin Knights / 双胞胎骑士 （Page 5）

中文: （a）8 名实力相同的骑士参加单败淘汰赛，其中有一对双胞胎。他们在比赛中相遇的概率是多少？（b）若参赛者共有 $2^n$ 人，答案是多少？

English: (a) Eight evenly matched knights play a single-elimination tournament, including a specified pair of twins. What is the probability that the twins meet? (b) What is the answer with $2^n$ players?

Solution

Fix one twin's bracket position. Of the other $2^n-1$ slots, exactly $2^{k-1}$ would make the twins first eligible to meet in round $k$. They must then each survive $k-1$ fair matches, an event of probability $2^{-2(k-1)}$. Therefore

$$P(\text{twins meet})=\frac{1}{2^n-1}\sum_{k=1}^{n}2^{k-1}2^{-2(k-1)}=\frac{2(1-2^{-n})}{2^n-1}=2^{1-n}.$$

For eight players, $n=3$, so the answer is $1/4$.

M018 · Problem 18 An Even Split at Coin Tossing / 百次抛硬币恰好平分 （Page 5）

中文: 抛 100 次公平硬币，恰好出现 50 次正面的概率是多少？

English: One hundred fair coins are tossed. What is the probability of obtaining exactly 50 heads?

Solution

The exact probability is

$$P=\frac{\binom{100}{50}}{2^{100}}\approx0.0795892.$$

More generally, Stirling's approximation gives

$$\frac{\binom{2m}{m}}{4^m}\sim\frac1{\sqrt{\pi m}}.$$

With $m=50$, this approximation is $1/\sqrt{50\pi}\approx0.0797885$.

M019 · Problem 19 Isaac Newton Helps Samuel Pepys / 牛顿帮助佩皮斯 （Page 6）

中文: 比较三个事件：（a）掷 6 枚骰子至少出现 1 个 6；（b）掷 12 枚骰子至少出现 2 个 6；（c）掷 18 枚骰子至少出现 3 个 6。哪个概率最大？

English: Which is most likely: (a) at least one six in 6 dice, (b) at least two sixes in 12 dice, or (c) at least three sixes in 18 dice?

Solution

For $X\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n,1/6)$, subtract the probabilities below the required count:

$$P_a=1-\left(\frac56\right)^6\approx0.665102,$$

$$P_b=1-\left[\left(\frac56\right)^{12}+12\frac16\left(\frac56\right)^{11}\right]\approx0.618667,$$

$$P_c=1-\sum_{j=0}^{2}\binom{18}{j}\left(\frac16\right)^j\left(\frac56\right)^{18-j}\approx0.597346.$$

Thus event (a) is the most likely.

M020 · Problem 20 The Three-Cornered Duel / 三人决斗 （Page 6）

中文: A、B、C 依次循环开枪，可选择任一仍在场的对手；中弹者退出。A、C 的命中率分别为 $0.3$ 和 $0.5$，B 百发百中，A 先开枪。A 应采取什么策略？

English: A, B, and C fire cyclically in that order, choosing any surviving opponent; a hit eliminates the target. A hits with probability $0.3$, C with $0.5$, and B never misses. A fires first. What should A do?

Solution

A should deliberately miss. B then eliminates C, the more dangerous opponent, and A gets one shot at B, winning with probability $0.3$.

If A shoots C, only a miss followed by B's elimination of C lets A survive, giving $0.7(0.3)=0.21$. If A shoots B, a miss gives $0.7(0.3)$; after a hit, C shoots first in the A–C duel, so A's eventual win chance is

$$\frac{(1-0.5)0.3}{1-(1-0.5)(1-0.3)}=\frac3{13}.$$

The total is $0.7(0.3)+0.3(3/13)\approx0.27923<0.3$. Intentional inaction is optimal.

M021 · Problem 21 Should You Sample with or without Replacement? / 应该放回抽样吗？ （Page 6）

中文: 罐 A 有 2 个红球、1 个黑球，罐 B 有 101 个红球、100 个黑球。等概率随机选一只罐，从中抽两球并据此判断是哪只罐。第一球颜色公布后，你可决定放回或不放回，再抽第二球。怎样决策？

English: Urn A contains two red and one black ball; urn B contains 101 red and 100 black balls. One urn is chosen at random. After seeing the first draw, you may choose whether to replace it before the second draw, then identify the urn. What policy should you use?

Solution

If the first ball is red, draw with replacement. Without replacement both urns would give red next with probability $1/2$, so the second draw would be uninformative; with replacement the probabilities are $2/3$ and $101/201$.

If the first ball is black, draw without replacement. Urn A must then produce red, whereas urn B produces red with probability $101/200$.

Under these choices, identify A if the second ball is red and B if it is black. The likelihood comparisons confirm both decisions. The resulting success probability is

$$\frac12\left[\frac79+\frac{40099}{80802}\right]=\frac{34315}{53868}\approx0.63702.$$

M022 · Problem 22 The Ballot Box / 选票箱问题 （Page 6）

中文: 候选人 Albert 和 Benjamin 分别得到 $a$ 与 $b$ 张票，其中 $a>b$。选票以随机顺序逐张唱票。第一次唱票之后，某个时刻两人的累计票数至少一次相等的概率是多少？

English: Candidates Albert and Benjamin receive $a$ and $b$ votes, with $a>b$. Ballots are counted in a uniformly random order. What is the probability that their running totals are tied at least once after the first ballot?

Solution

Bertrand's ballot theorem says that the probability Albert remains strictly ahead throughout the count is

$$\frac{a-b}{a+b}.$$

This is exactly the complement of having a tie after the first ballot. Therefore

$$P(\text{at least one tie})=1-\frac{a-b}{a+b}=\boxed{\frac{2b}{a+b}}.$$

M023 · Problem 23 Ties in Matching Pennies / 猜硬币游戏中的平局 （Page 6）

中文: A 与 B 连续玩 $N$ 局猜硬币，每局恰有一人得 1 分。第一局结束后，在余下过程中双方累计得分再也不相等的概率是多少？

English: A and B play matching pennies for $N$ rounds, with exactly one point awarded per round. After the first round, what is the probability that their cumulative scores are never tied during the rest of the game?

Solution

Condition on the first winner and track the score difference as a simple symmetric random walk starting at $1$. By the reflection principle, the number of length-$(N-1)$ continuations that never hit zero is

$$\binom{N-1}{\lfloor (N-1)/2\rfloor}.$$

Since all $2^{N-1}$ continuations are equally likely,

$$P(\text{no later tie})=\boxed{\frac{\binom{N-1}{\lfloor (N-1)/2\rfloor}}{2^{N-1}}}.$$

For even $N=2m$, this is equivalently $\binom{2m}{m}/2^{2m}$.

M024 · Problem 24 The Unfair Subway / 不公平的地铁 （Page 7）

中文: Marvin 每天下午 3 点至 5 点随机下班，母亲住在上城，女友住在下城。他总乘最先到站的列车，却连续 20 个工作日都去了女友家。他声称两个方向机会各半。问题出在哪里？

English: Marvin leaves work at a random time between 3 and 5 p.m. His mother lives uptown and his girlfriend downtown. He takes the first train to arrive, yet has gone downtown on each of the last 20 workdays. Why need the two directions not have equal probability?

Solution

Equal train frequencies do not imply equal waiting-time catchment intervals. For example, suppose downtown trains arrive at 3:00, 3:10, 3:20, ... and uptown trains at 3:01, 3:11, 3:21, .... During each ten-minute cycle, only the one-minute interval after a downtown train leads to the uptown train; the other nine minutes lead to downtown. Marvin then goes downtown with probability $0.9$ on each day. The schedule, not merely the number of trains, determines the direction probability.

M025 · Problem 25 Lengths of Random Chords / 随机弦的长度 （Page 7）

中文: 在一个固定圆中“随机”选一条弦。弦长超过圆半径的概率是多少？

English: A chord is selected “at random” in a fixed circle. What is the probability that its length exceeds the circle's radius?

Solution

The phrase “at random” does not specify a probability measure, so there is no unique answer. For radius $R$, the chord is longer than $R$ exactly when its midpoint lies within distance $\sqrt3R/2$ of the center.

  • If the chord's distance from the center is uniform on $[0,R]$, the probability is $\sqrt3/2$.
  • If the midpoint is uniform over the disk, the probability is $(\sqrt3R/2)^2/R^2=3/4$.
  • If two endpoints are independently uniform on the circumference, the probability is $2/3$.

This is Bertrand's paradox: each answer is correct for its own sampling mechanism.

M026 · Problem 26 The Hurried Duelers / 匆忙的决斗者 （Page 7）

中文: 两名决斗者各自在清晨 5 点至 6 点之间独立均匀到达；每人到达后只等 5 分钟，若对方在此期间出现就交战，否则离开。发生交战的比例是多少？

English: Two duelers independently arrive at a uniformly random time between 5 and 6 a.m. Each waits exactly five minutes and fights if the other arrives during that interval. What fraction of duels lead to a fight?

Solution

Let arrival times $X,Y$ be uniform on the $60\times60$ minute square. A fight occurs when $|X-Y|\le5$. The complement consists of two right triangles of side $55$, so

$$P(\text{fight})=1-\frac{2(55^2/2)}{60^2}=1-\frac{3025}{3600}=\boxed{\frac{23}{144}}\approx0.15972.$$

M027 · Problem 27 Catching the Cautious Counterfeiter / 抓住谨慎的造假者 （Page 7）

中文: 造币者把硬币按每箱 100 枚装箱，每箱恰放 1 枚假币。国王从 100 箱中每箱随机抽 1 枚检验。（a）造假未被发现的概率是多少？（b）若两处的 100 都改为 $n$，答案是什么？

English: A minter packs 100 coins per box and puts exactly one counterfeit in every box. The king samples one coin from each of 100 boxes. (a) What is the probability that no counterfeit is found? (b) Replace both occurrences of 100 by $n$.

Solution

Each box independently escapes detection with probability $99/100$, so

$$P_{100}=\left(\frac{99}{100}\right)^{100}\approx0.36603.$$

In the general case,

$$P_n=\left(1-\frac1n\right)^n\longrightarrow e^{-1}\approx0.36788.$$

M028 · Problem 28 Catching the Greedy Counterfeiter / 抓住贪心的造假者 （Page 8）

中文: 造币者每箱装 $n$ 枚硬币，其中 $m$ 枚是假币；共有 $n$ 箱。国王从每箱随机抽 1 枚。样本中的假币恰好为 $r$ 枚的概率是多少？

English: Each of $n$ boxes contains $n$ coins, of which $m$ are counterfeit. The king draws one coin from each box. What is the probability that the sample contains exactly $r$ counterfeits?

Solution

Each sampled coin is counterfeit with probability $m/n$, independently across boxes. Therefore the count $R$ is binomial:

$$P(R=r)=\boxed{\binom nr\left(\frac mn\right)^r\left(1-\frac mn\right)^{n-r}},\qquad r=0,1,\ldots,n.$$

If $m$ is fixed and $n$ is large, this converges to the Poisson probability $e^{-m}m^r/r!$.

M029 · Problem 29 Moldy Gelatin / 发霉的明胶培养皿 （Page 8）

English: Airborne spores produce mold colonies on laboratory plates, averaging three colonies per plate. What fraction have exactly three colonies? If the mean is a large integer $m$, what fraction have exactly $m$?

Solution

Under the Poisson model, $X\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(3)$, so

$$P(X=3)=e^{-3}\frac{3^3}{3!}=\frac92e^{-3}\approx0.22404.$$

For mean $m$,

$$P(X=m)=e^{-m}\frac{m^m}{m!}\sim\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi m}},$$

M030 · Problem 30 Evening the Sales / 销量为偶数的概率 （Page 8）

中文: 面包销售员每轮平均卖出 20 个蛋糕，销量服从 Poisson 分布。卖出偶数个蛋糕的概率是多少？

English: A bread salesman sells an average of 20 cakes per round, with sales modeled by a Poisson distribution. What is the probability of selling an even number?

Solution

For $X\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda)$, use $E[(-1)^X]=e^{-2\lambda}$ and $P(\text{even})+P(\text{odd})=1$. Hence

$$P(X\text{ even})=\frac{1+e^{-2\lambda}}2.$$

At $\lambda=20$, the answer is

$$\boxed{\frac{1+e^{-40}}2}\approx0.5.$$

M031 · Problem 31 Birthday Pairings / 生日配对 （Page 8）

中文: 忽略闰日，并假定 365 天等可能且各人的生日独立。至少需要多少人，才能使其中至少两人同生日的概率超过 $1/2$？

English: Ignore leap day and assume independent uniformly distributed birthdays over 365 days. What is the smallest group size for which the probability of at least one shared birthday exceeds $1/2$?

Solution

For $n\le365$, the probability of no shared birthday is

$$P_0(n)=\prod_{k=0}^{n-1}\frac{365-k}{365}.$$

Direct evaluation gives $1-P_0(22)\approx0.4757$ and $1-P_0(23)\approx0.5073$. Thus the smallest group is $23$ people.

M032 · Problem 32 Finding Your Birthmate / 寻找同生日的人 （Page 8）

中文: 你想找到一名与自己同生日的人。仍假设 365 天等可能、生日彼此独立。至少要询问多少个陌生人，成功概率才不低于 $1/2$？

English: You seek someone whose birthday matches yours. Under the same independent uniform 365-day model, how many strangers must you ask to have at least a 50–50 chance?

Solution

Each stranger fails to match with probability $364/365$. We need

$$1-\left(\frac{364}{365}\right)^n\ge\frac12,$$

or

$$n\ge\frac{\log(1/2)}{\log(364/365)}\approx252.65.$$

Therefore the minimum is $253$ strangers.

M033 · Problem 33 Relating Birthday Pairings and Birthmates / 两类生日问题的关系 （Page 9）

中文: 在生日配对问题中，$r$ 人里至少两人同生日的概率为 $P_r$。在“寻找与你同生日的人”问题中，应询问多少人 $n$，才能使成功概率近似为 $P_r$？

English: In the birthday-pairing problem, let $P_r$ be the probability that at least two among $r$ people share a birthday. How large should $n$ be in the personal birthmate problem to obtain approximately the same success probability?

Solution

The exact matching choice is

$$n=\frac{\log(1-P_r)}{\log(364/365)},$$

rounded to a suitable integer. For moderate $r$,

$$1-P_r\approx\exp\left[-\frac{r(r-1)}{2\cdot365}\right],\qquad \left(\frac{364}{365}\right)^n\approx e^{-n/365}.$$

Equating the exponents gives the useful relation

$$\boxed{n\approx\frac{r(r-1)}2}.$$

M034 · Problem 34 Birthday Holidays / 生日假期 （Page 9）

中文: Erewhon 的工厂中，只要任一工人过生日，全体工人当天放假；除这些生日假外，一年 365 天都工作。雇主不能按生日歧视招聘。为了最大化工厂每年的期望总人天数，应雇多少名工人？

English: In Erewhon, every factory closes whenever any employee has a birthday; otherwise it operates all 365 days. Hiring cannot discriminate by birthday. How many workers maximize the expected total worker-days per year?

Solution

With $N$ workers, a given day is a workday only if none has that birthday, with probability $(364/365)^N$. Thus

$$W_N=365N\left(\frac{364}{365}\right)^N.$$

The ratio

$$\frac{W_{N+1}}{W_N}=\frac{N+1}{N}\frac{364}{365}$$

is greater than $1$ for $N<364$, equals $1$ at $N=364$, and is less than $1$ afterward. Therefore $N=364$ and $N=365$ tie for the maximum.

M035 · Problem 35 The Cliff-Hanger / 悬崖边的醉汉 （Page 9）

中文: 一名醉汉站在离悬崖边一步的位置。每一步以概率 $2/3$ 远离悬崖，以概率 $1/3$ 走向悬崖；走到边缘即坠落。无限行走时，他逃脱悬崖的概率是多少？

English: A drunk starts one step from a cliff. Each step moves away from the cliff with probability $2/3$ and toward it with probability $1/3$; reaching the edge is fatal. What is the probability of escaping forever?

Solution

For a biased random walk with upward probability $p>q$, the probability of ever hitting zero from state $i$ is $(q/p)^i$. Here $i=1$, $p=2/3$, and $q=1/3$, so

$$P(\text{fall})=\frac{q}{p}=\frac12.$$

Therefore

$$P(\text{escape})=1-P(\text{fall})=\boxed{\frac12}.$$

M036 · Problem 36 Gambler’s Ruin / 赌徒破产 （Page 9）

中文: M 有 1 美元，N 有 2 美元。每局赢家从输家处获得 1 美元，M 每局以概率 $2/3$ 获胜。两人一直玩到一人破产。M 最终赢得全部 3 美元的概率是多少？

English: M starts with \$1 and N with \$2. Each play transfers one dollar from loser to winner, and M wins each play with probability $2/3$. They continue until one is bankrupt. What is the probability that M wins all \$3?

Solution

For gambler's ruin with target $K$, initial fortune $i$, win probability $p$, and loss probability $q$,

$$u_i=\frac{1-(q/p)^i}{1-(q/p)^K},\qquad p\ne q.$$

Here $K=3$, $i=1$, and $q/p=1/2$, so

$$u_1=\frac{1-1/2}{1-(1/2)^3}=\boxed{\frac47}.$$

M037 · Problem 37 Bold Play vs. Cautious Play / 豪赌与谨慎下注 （Page 9）

中文: 一名赌客有 20 美元，目标是赢到 40 美元。他考虑两种美式轮盘“押偶数”策略：一次押上全部 20 美元；或者每次押 1 美元，直到资产变为 0 或 40。比较两种策略达到目标的概率。

English: A gambler has \$20 and needs \$40. On an American roulette even-money bet, compare staking all \$20 at once with betting \$1 repeatedly until reaching either \$0 or \$40.

Solution

An even-money roulette bet wins with $p=18/38=9/19$ and loses with $q=10/19$. Bold play succeeds with probability

$$P_{\mathrm{bold}}=\frac9{19}\approx0.47368.$$

Cautious play is gambler's ruin starting at $20$ with barriers $0$ and $40$:

$$P_{\mathrm{cautious}}=\frac{1-(q/p)^{20}}{1-(q/p)^{40}}=\frac{1}{1+(10/9)^{20}}\approx0.10840.$$

Bold play is much better because it minimizes exposure to the house edge.

M038 · Problem 38 The Thick Coin / 厚硬币 （Page 10）

中文: 一枚硬币应该有多厚，才会有 $1/3$ 的概率立在边缘？

English: How thick should a coin be to have probability $1/3$ of landing on its edge?

Solution

A real answer depends on elasticity, launch, and surface physics. Under the standard idealized model, regard the coin as a cylinder of radius $r$ and half-thickness $h$, inscribed in a sphere, and take the final downward direction uniformly on the sphere. Edge landings correspond to the equatorial zone bounded by rays through the rims. Its area fraction is

$$P(\text{edge})=\frac{h}{\sqrt{r^2+h^2}}.$$

Setting this to $1/3$ gives $r^2=8h^2$. Thus the full thickness $t=2h$ must satisfy

$$t=\frac{r}{\sqrt2}=\frac{d}{2\sqrt2}\approx0.3536d,$$

where $d=2r$ is the diameter.

M039 · Problem 39 The Clumsy Chemist / 笨手笨脚的化学师 （Page 10）

中文: 若干根 9 英寸长的细玻璃棒一端标蓝点、另一端标红点。它们随机摔成三段。带蓝点那一段的平均长度是多少？

English: Thin nine-inch glass rods have a blue mark at one end and a red mark at the other. They break at two random points into three pieces. What is the average length of the blue-ended fragment?

Solution

The two random breakpoints create three exchangeable spacings: the blue-end piece, the middle piece, and the red-end piece. Their expected lengths are equal and must sum to $9$ inches. Therefore each has mean

$$\boxed{9/3=3\text{ inches}}.$$

M040 · Problem 40 The First Ace / 第一张 A （Page 10）

中文: 将含 4 张 A 的标准 52 张扑克牌充分洗匀，从牌顶逐张翻开，直到第一张 A 出现。平均要翻多少张？

English: Shuffle a standard 52-card deck containing four aces and reveal cards until the first ace appears. What is the expected number of cards revealed?

Solution

The four aces divide the 48 non-aces into five exchangeable gaps: before the first ace, between consecutive aces, and after the last. The expected number of non-aces in the first gap is $48/5$. Including the first ace,

$$\mathbb E[T]=1+\frac{48}{5}=\boxed{\frac{53}{5}}=10.6.$$

M041 · Problem 41 The Locomotive Problem / 火车头编号估计 （Page 10）

中文: 某铁路公司的火车头依次编号为 $1,2,\ldots,N$。（a）随机看到一台，编号为 60，估计公司共有多少台？（b）随机看到 5 台，其中最大编号为 60，再次估计 $N$。

English: A railroad numbers its locomotives $1,2,\ldots,N$. (a) One randomly observed locomotive is numbered 60; estimate $N$. (b) Five are observed and the largest number is 60; estimate $N$ again.

Solution

An estimate requires a criterion; there is no uniquely “correct” guess from the data alone. A simple unbiased estimator from one discrete uniform observation $X$ is $2X-1$, because $E[X]=(N+1)/2$. Thus part (a) gives

$$\widehat N=2(60)-1=119.$$

For $k$ draws without replacement with observed maximum $M$, the unbiased German-tank estimator is

$$\widehat N=M+\frac{M}{k}-1.$$

With $M=60$ and $k=5$, part (b) gives $71$. By contrast, maximum likelihood gives $\widehat N=60$; a Bayesian estimate would depend on its prior. Stating the estimation principle is essential.

M042 · Problem 42 The Little End of the Stick / 木棍较短的一端 （Page 10）

中文: 一根木棍在均匀随机位置折成两段。（a）较短一段的平均长度占原长多少？（b）较短段与较长段之比的期望是多少？

English: A stick is broken at a uniformly random point. (a) What is the expected length of the shorter piece as a fraction of the original length? (b) What is the expected ratio of the shorter piece to the longer?

Solution

Normalize the stick to length $1$ and let $X\sim U(0,1)$ be the break point. Then

$$E[\min(X,1-X)]=2\int_0^{1/2}x\,dx=\boxed{\frac14}.$$

For the ratio, symmetry gives

$$E\left[\frac{\min(X,1-X)}{\max(X,1-X)}\right]=2\int_{1/2}^{1}\frac{1-x}{x}\,dx=\boxed{2\log2-1}\approx0.38629.$$

M043 · Problem 43 The Broken Bar / 随机折断的长条 （Page 11）

Solution

Normalize the bar to length $1$. The three spacings have the Dirichlet$(1,1,1)$ distribution. Their ordered expectations are

$$E[L_{(1)}]=\boxed{\frac19},\qquad E[L_{(2)}]=\boxed{\frac5{18}},\qquad E[L_{(3)}]=\boxed{\frac{11}{18}}.$$

M044 · Problem 44 Winning an Unfair Game / 如何赢下不公平的比赛 （Page 11）

中文: 每局你以概率 $p<1/2$ 得 1 分，对手以概率 $1-p$ 得 1 分。总局数必须预先选为正偶数；总分过半者获奖。若 $p=0.45$，应选多少局？一般怎样选择？

English: On each play you score with probability $p<1/2$ and your opponent otherwise scores. You must choose a positive even number of plays in advance and win only by taking more than half the points. For $p=0.45$, how many plays should you choose, and what is the general rule?

Solution

For $N=2n$ plays,

$$P_{2n}=\sum_{k=n+1}^{2n}\binom{2n}{k}p^k(1-p)^{2n-k}.$$

Comparing $P_{2n}$ with its two even neighbors shows that an optimum satisfies

$$\frac{1}{1-2p}-1\le 2n\le\frac{1}{1-2p}+1.$$

Thus choose the even integer nearest to $1/(1-2p)$; if that number is an odd integer, the two adjacent even choices tie. For $p=0.45$,

$$\frac{1}{1-2p}=10,$$

so the unique optimum is $10$ plays.

M045 · Problem 45 Average Number of Matches / 匹配数的期望 （Page 11）

中文: （a）把两副各含 $n$ 张不同牌的牌洗匀后上下对应排放，平均有多少个位置牌面相同？（b）把写给 $n$ 个不同收件人的信随机装入 $n$ 个已写地址的信封，平均有多少封装对？

English: (a) Two independently shuffled decks of $n$ distinct cards are aligned position by position. What is the expected number of matches? (b) Letters for $n$ people are randomly placed into the $n$ addressed envelopes. What is the expected number correctly matched?

Solution

In either version, let $I_i$ indicate that item $i$ is correctly matched. Since $P(I_i=1)=1/n$, linearity of expectation gives

$$E\left[\sum_{i=1}^n I_i\right]=\sum_{i=1}^n E[I_i]=n\cdot\frac1n=\boxed{1}.$$

The indicators are dependent, but independence is not required for linearity of expectation.

M046 · Problem 46 Probabilities of Matches / 恰好匹配若干项的概率 （Page 12）

中文: 延续上一题，在 $n$ 个对象的随机排列中，恰好有 $r$ 个对象留在原位的概率是多少？

English: Under the matching setup above, what is the probability that exactly $r$ of the $n$ objects occupy their correct positions?

Solution

Choose the $r$ fixed objects and derange the remaining $n-r$. If $D_m=m!\sum_{j=0}^{m}(-1)^j/j!$ is the number of derangements of $m$ objects, then

$$P(R=r)=\frac{\binom nrD_{n-r}}{n!}=\boxed{\frac1{r!}\sum_{j=0}^{n-r}\frac{(-1)^j}{j!}}.$$

For fixed $r$ and large $n-r$, this approaches $e^{-1}/r!$, the Poisson$(1)$ probability.

M047 · Problem 47 Choosing the Largest Dowry / 选择最大嫁妆 （Page 12）

中文: 100 个互不相同的嫁妆数额按随机顺序逐一出现。每次只能知道当前数额与此前数额的相对大小，必须立即接受或永久拒绝，且只能接受一次。怎样最大化选中全局最大值的概率？

English: One hundred distinct dowries arrive in random order. At each draw you know only its rank relative to earlier draws and must accept or reject it immediately, accepting at most once. How should you maximize the chance of selecting the largest?

Solution

Reject the first $r$ observations, then accept the first later record. This policy succeeds with probability

$$P(r)=\frac{r}{100}\sum_{k=r+1}^{100}\frac1{k-1}.$$

The maximizing cutoff is $r=37$: reject the first 37, then take the first subsequent value larger than all preceding values. The success probability is about $0.3710$. In general, $r\sim n/e$ and the optimal probability tends to $1/e$.

M048 · Problem 48 Choosing the Largest Random Number / 选择最大的随机数 （Page 12）

中文: 仍从 100 个依次出现的数中选择全局最大值，但现在已知这些数独立服从 $U(0,1)$，且能看到实际数值。应采用什么策略？

English: Again choose the largest of 100 sequentially observed values, but now the values are independent $U(0,1)$ random variables and their actual magnitudes are visible. What strategy should be used?

Solution

Only a current record can be accepted. Work backward and use a different threshold for each number of remaining observations. If a record $x$ is in hand with $r$ observations still to come, accept it exactly when $x\ge a_r$, where $a_r$ is the solution in $(0,1)$ of

$$a_r^r=\sum_{j=1}^{r}\frac1j\binom rj a_r^{\,r-j}(1-a_r)^j.$$

The first thresholds are $a_1=0.5$, $a_2=(1+\sqrt6)/5\approx0.68990$, $a_3\approx0.77585$, and they increase backward toward the start. For large $r$,

$$a_r\approx\frac{r}{r+0.8043}.$$

Thus the first draw, with $r=99$ future draws, needs to exceed roughly $0.99194$ to be accepted. If no earlier choice is made, accept the final observation only if it is a record. This backward-threshold policy uses the known distribution and outperforms the rank-only rule.

M049 · Problem 49 Doubling Your Accuracy / 加倍测量精度 （Page 12）

中文: 一台无偏长度测量仪的随机误差标准差为 $\sigma$。现有两根圆柱棒，其中一根明显更长；总共只允许测量两次。能否比“每根各量一次”更准确地估计两根长度？

English: An unbiased length instrument has random error with standard deviation $\sigma$. Two rods have visibly different lengths, and only two measurements are allowed. Can both lengths be estimated more accurately than by measuring each rod once?

Solution

Let the true lengths be $A>B$. Measure the aligned difference $D=A-B+d$ and the end-to-end sum $S=A+B+s$, where $d$ and $s$ are independent errors with mean zero and variance $\sigma^2$. Then use

$$\widehat A=\frac{S+D}{2},\qquad \widehat B=\frac{S-D}{2}.$$

Both estimators are unbiased, and

$$\operatorname{Var}(\widehat A)=\operatorname{Var}(\widehat B)=\frac14(\sigma^2+\sigma^2)=\frac{\sigma^2}{2}.$$

Thus each rod is estimated as accurately as if it had been measured twice and averaged: two physical measurements deliver the precision normally requiring four. This assumes sum and difference can be aligned without extra systematic error.

M050 · Problem 50 Random Quadratic Equations / 随机二次方程 （Page 12）

中文: 随机二次方程 $x^2+2bx+c=0$ 有实根的概率是多少？请先明确“随机系数”的分布模型。

English: What is the probability that the random quadratic equation $x^2+2bx+c=0$ has real roots? First specify what “random coefficients” means.

Solution

Without a distribution for $(b,c)$, the question has no unique answer. Use the book's model: choose $(b,c)$ uniformly from the square $[-B,B]^2$, with $B\ge1$. Real roots require $b^2-c\ge0$. The complex-root region is $c>b^2$, whose area is

$$\int_{-\sqrt B}^{\sqrt B}(B-b^2)\,db=\frac43B^{3/2}.$$

Dividing by the square's area $4B^2$ gives

$$P(\text{real roots})=\boxed{1-\frac{1}{3\sqrt B}}.$$

It equals $5/6$ when $B=4$ and tends to $1$ as $B\to\infty$. Other coefficient distributions give other answers.

M051 · Problem 51 Two-Dimensional Random Walk / 二维随机游走 （Page 13）

中文: 粒子从原点出发。每一步的南北方向以各 $1/2$ 选择，同时东西方向也以各 $1/2$ 选择，因此等概率走向四个对角格点之一。无限行走时，粒子至少一次回到原点的概率是多少？

English: A particle starts at the origin. On every move it independently chooses north or south with equal probability and east or west with equal probability, so it moves to one of four diagonal lattice neighbors. What is the probability of ever returning to the origin?

Solution

After $2n$ moves, each coordinate returns to zero with probability $\binom{2n}{n}/2^{2n}$, independently, so

$$P(S_{2n}=0)=\left[\frac{\binom{2n}{n}}{2^{2n}}\right]^2\sim\frac1{\pi n}.$$

The sum $\sum_{n\ge1}P(S_{2n}=0)$ diverges like the harmonic series. The expected number of visits is therefore infinite, which for this irreducible walk implies recurrence. Hence

$$P(\text{ever return})=\boxed{1}.$$

In fact the walk returns infinitely often with probability one.

M052 · Problem 52 Three-Dimensional Random Walk / 三维随机游走 （Page 13）

中文: 粒子从三维原点出发，每一步在三个坐标上分别独立地取 $+1$ 或 $-1$，即等概率走向以当前位置为中心、边长 2 的立方体的八个角之一。无限行走时，至少一次回到原点的概率是多少？

English: A particle starts at the origin in three dimensions. At every move, each coordinate independently changes by $+1$ or $-1$, so the particle moves uniformly to one of the eight corners of a cube centered at its current position. What is the probability of ever returning to the origin?

Solution

At time $2n$, all three independent coordinates must return to zero:

$$u_{2n}=\left[\frac{\binom{2n}{n}}{2^{2n}}\right]^3.$$

Let $G=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}u_{2n}$ be the expected total number of visits including time zero. Renewal theory gives $G=1/(1-F)$, where $F$ is the probability of at least one return. Accurate evaluation yields

$$G=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\binom{2n}{n}^3}{64^n}\approx1.3932039297,$$

and hence

$$F=1-\frac1G\approx\boxed{0.2822299890}.$$

The series converges because $u_{2n}\sim(\pi n)^{-3/2}$, so the three-dimensional walk is transient.

M053 · Problem 53 Buffon’s Needle / Buffon 投针 （Page 14）

中文: 平面上画有间距 $2a$ 的平行线。随机抛下一根长度为 $2\ell<2a$ 的针，针的方向均匀，针中心相对线的位置也均匀。针与某条线相交的概率是多少？

English: Parallel lines are spaced $2a$ apart. A needle of length $2\ell<2a$ is tossed with uniform orientation and uniform center position relative to the lines. What is the probability that it crosses a line?

Solution

If $\theta\in[0,\pi/2]$ is the acute angle between the needle and the lines, a crossing occurs when the center is within $\ell\sin\theta$ of a line. The conditional probability is $(\ell/a)\sin\theta$. Averaging over uniform $\theta$ gives

$$P=\frac{2}{\pi}\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{\ell}{a}\sin\theta\,d\theta=\boxed{\frac{2\ell}{\pi a}}.$$

M054 · Problem 54 Buffon’s Needle with Horizontal and Vertical Rulings / 横纵网格上的 Buffon 投针 （Page 14）

中文: 在横线与竖线间距都为 1 的方格纸上随机抛一根长度 $2\ell<1$ 的针。针平均会穿过多少条网格线？

English: A needle of length $2\ell<1$ is tossed on a square grid whose horizontal and vertical lines are one unit apart. What is the expected number of grid lines crossed?

Solution

For the vertical-line family, Buffon's formula with spacing $1$ gives expected crossings $2(2\ell)/(\pi\cdot1)=4\ell/\pi$. The horizontal family contributes the same amount. By linearity,

$$E[N_{\mathrm{cross}}]=\boxed{\frac{8\ell}{\pi}}.$$

For a needle of length $1$ ($\ell=1/2$), the mean is $4/\pi\approx1.2732$.

M055 · Problem 55 Long Needles / 长针 （Page 14）

中文: 延续上一题，但允许针的长度任意。针平均穿过多少条单位方格网格线？

English: In the preceding unit square grid, let the needle have arbitrary length. What is the expected number of line crossings?

Solution

Let the full needle length be $L$. For a fixed angle $\theta$, its horizontal projection crosses an expected $L|\cos\theta|$ vertical lines and its vertical projection crosses an expected $L|\sin\theta|$ horizontal lines. Averaging over uniform orientation,

$$E[N]=L\left(E|\cos\theta|+E|\sin\theta|\right)=L\left(\frac2\pi+\frac2\pi\right)=\boxed{\frac{4L}{\pi}}.$$

With the book's notation $L=2\ell$, this is $8\ell/\pi$. The formula remains valid for long needles because it counts multiple crossings.

M056 · Problem 56 Molina’s Urns / Molina 的罐子 （Page 14）

中文: 两只罐子的球总数相同，且每只罐中都有黑球和白球。从每只罐中有放回地抽 $n\ge3$ 次。能否安排 $n$ 与两罐组成，使“第一罐全抽到白球”的概率等于“第二罐全白或全黑”的概率？

English: Two urns contain the same total number of balls, with both colors present in each. From each urn, draw $n\ge3$ times with replacement. Can the number of draws and the urn compositions be chosen so that the probability of all white from the first urn equals the probability of either all white or all black from the second?

Solution

Let the second urn contain $x$ white and $y$ black balls, so the common total is $x+y$, and let the first contain $z$ white balls. The required equality is

$$\left(\frac{z}{x+y}\right)^n=\left(\frac{x}{x+y}\right)^n+\left(\frac{y}{x+y}\right)^n,$$

or, after clearing denominators,

$$z^n=x^n+y^n.$$

Because $x,y,z$ are positive integers and $n\ge3$, Fermat's Last Theorem says this equation has no nonzero integer solutions. Therefore no such nontrivial urn compositions exist. The problem, devised before Fermat's Last Theorem was proved, is now completely settled.
